# Keys
<!-- help: keys; tui -->

The same keys work on every sheet. The footer shows the ones that work where
you are: on its left what the focused part of the sheet can do, on its right
the keys that always work.

### Acting on what has focus
<!-- help: keys-acting -->

- `a` adds to the focused list, `e` edits its highlighted row (or the focused
  section, or the record), `d` deletes the highlighted row after asking first.
- `m` More holds the rarer actions for where you are. With one action it runs
  it straight away and the footer names it; with several it shows a menu.
- `n` New makes a covenant, character, laboratory or familiar bond, wherever
  that can be made from here.
- `/` filters a list that can be filtered (Spells, Lab Texts).

### Moving around
<!-- help: keys-moving -->

- `Tab` and `Shift+Tab` move from section to section; the arrow keys move
  within a list.
- `1` to `9` open a sheet's tabs, and `0` the tenth; the tabs show their numbers.
- `Enter` on the tree moves into the sheet it shows; `esc` steps back out:
  from the sheet to the tree, then to the saga picker.
- `c` collapses or expands the focused section.
- A mouse click on a section focuses it; a second click on its title
  collapses or expands it.

### Always there
<!-- help: keys-always -->

- `t` Advance Season (see [Advancing a season](help:advance-season)).
- `Ctrl+F` Find: type a name and open that record, wherever it is in the
  saga (see [Find](help:find)).
- `?` Help, here. In a dialog where you're typing, `F1` opens it instead.
- `q` Quit, and `Ctrl+P` the command palette (themes and more).

In Help itself, the arrow keys choose a topic, `/` filters the topics,
`Tab` moves between the topic list and the page, `g` (Go there) closes it
and opens the tab or section the topic is about, and `esc` closes it.

# Keys
<!-- help: keys; gui -->

The desktop version is used with the mouse: every action is a button on the page, in the chapter or section it acts on. A few keys work too, the same ones as in the terminal.

### Always there
<!-- help: keys-always; gui -->

- `?` or `F1` opens Help on the page on show, as the top bar's **Help** does.
- `Esc` cancels the form or question that is open, as its Cancel does; with none open, it goes back a page, to the one above in the trail (from Help, to the page you opened it on).
- `q` quits Armarius. Everything is already saved.
- `Ctrl+F` drops **Find** from the top bar, to open any record by its name (see [Find](help:find)).
- `?` and `q` are typed as letters while you are typing in a box, and do nothing while a form or question is open, so they never act by accident. `Esc` and `F1` always work.
- **Advance Season**, under the chapters on the left, advances the saga by one season (see [Advancing a season](help:advance-season)).
- The trail at the top left goes back to any page above this one.

### In a form
<!-- help: keys-forms; gui -->

- `Tab` and `Shift+Tab` move from field to field.
- `Enter` saves the form, as its Save button does; in a question that deletes something, Enter is No.
- `Esc` cancels the form or question, as Cancel does.

### On the title page and in Help
<!-- help: keys-elsewhere; gui -->

- On the title page, `Enter` takes the first way on (Open Saga, or All Sagas), `?` opens Help and `q` quits.
- In Help, type in the filter box to narrow the topics, and click a topic to show it. A link in the page shows its topic; **Go there** (under the list, or a Go there link in a guide) goes back to the page Help was opened from at the chapter the topic is about; `Esc`, or the trail, leads back to that page as it was.

See [The window](help:workspace) for what the buttons act on.
