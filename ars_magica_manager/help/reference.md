# Keys
<!-- help: keys -->

The same keys work on every sheet. The footer shows the ones that work where
you are: on its left what the focused part of the sheet can do, on its right
the keys that always work.

### Acting on what has focus
<!-- help: keys-acting -->

- `a` adds to the focused list, `e` edits its highlighted row (or the focused
  section, or the record), `d` deletes the highlighted row after asking first.
- `m` More holds the rarer actions for where you are. With one action it runs
  it straight away and the footer names it; with several it shows a menu.
- `n` New makes a covenant, character, laboratory or familiar bond, wherever
  that can be made from here.
- `/` filters a list that can be filtered (Spells, Lab Texts).

### Moving around
<!-- help: keys-moving -->

- `Tab` and `Shift+Tab` move from section to section; the arrow keys move
  within a list.
- `1` to `9` open a sheet's tabs; the tabs show their numbers.
- `Enter` on the tree moves into the sheet it shows; `esc` steps back out:
  from the sheet to the tree, then to the saga picker.
- `c` collapses or expands the focused section.
- A mouse click on a section focuses it; a second click on its title
  collapses or expands it.

### Always there
<!-- help: keys-always -->

- `t` Advance Season (see [Advancing a season](help:advance-season)).
- `?` Help, here. In a dialog where you're typing, `F1` opens it instead.
- `q` Quit, and `Ctrl+P` the command palette (themes and more).

In Help itself, the arrow keys choose a topic, `/` filters the topics,
`Tab` moves between the topic list and the page, and `esc` closes it.
