# How to plan and run a season
<!-- help: guide-seasons -->

The troupe has played out the spring's stories, and it's time to say what everyone did with the season and move the saga on to summer.

Nothing changes while you plan. Every plan waits until the season is advanced, and is applied then.

### Who is planned where
<!-- help: guide-seasons-who -->

<!-- tui -->
- **A magus's lab work**, such as inventing a spell, enchanting or a Longevity Ritual, is planned on their laboratory's [Planning tab](help:tab-lab-planning).
- **A magus's other activities**, such as reading, practice or teaching, and **every season of a companion or Mythic Companion**, are planned on the character's [Planning tab](help:tab-planning) or the covenant's [Seasons tab](help:tab-cov-seasons). Both open the same [Plan Season](help:plan-season) dialog.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
- **A magus's lab work**, such as inventing a spell, enchanting or a Longevity Ritual, is planned in the **Planning** section of their laboratory's **Seasons** chapter (see [Planning](help:tab-lab-planning)).
- **A magus's other activities**, such as reading, practice or teaching, and **every season of a companion or Mythic Companion**, are planned in the character's [Planning](help:tab-planning) chapter or the covenant's [Seasons](help:tab-cov-seasons) chapter. Both open the same [Plan Season](help:plan-season) form.
<!-- /gui -->
- **Grogs** aren't planned season by season. Each has a [yearly update](help:grog-year), applied when Winter is advanced.

Each magus, companion and Mythic Companion has one plan per season, wherever it was made.

### Planning a character's season
<!-- help: guide-seasons-plan -->

<!-- tui -->
1. **Open the character.** In the tree, open the covenant, then **Characters**, and choose the character. Press Enter to move into their sheet.
2. **Go to Planning.** Open the **Planning** tab. The **This Season** section names the season, or says "Nothing planned".
3. **Start a plan.** Press `e` (or `a`). The Plan Season dialog opens, titled with the character's name and the season.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
1. **Open the character.** On the saga's page, click the covenant's card, then the character's card in the **People** chapter. Their sheet opens.
2. **Go to Planning.** Choose **Planning** in the rail on the left. The **This Season** section names the season, or says "Nothing planned".
3. **Start a plan.** Click **Plan**, or **Edit** once a plan is made. The Plan Season form opens in a window, titled with the character's name and the season.
<!-- /gui -->
4. **Choose the Activity.** The list is grouped by Type, with the character's recent activities at the top. See [Finding an activity](help:guide-seasons-find).
5. **Fill in what it asks.** The form shows only the fields that activity needs, such as **Experience in**, a **Book** or a **Teacher / master**. Leave **Stress die (blank: rolled)** empty and the app rolls when the season is applied.
6. **Check the line above Save.** It shows what the season will give, or what's wrong with the plan. Save refuses a plan that breaks a rule until it's fixed.
7. **Save.** This Season now shows the plan and its expected gains.

<!-- tui -->
To change a plan, press `e` again. To clear it, press `d` and confirm.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
To change a plan, click **Edit**. To clear it, click **Clear** and confirm.
<!-- /gui -->

**Planning ahead.** **Seasons Ahead**, under This Season, lists the next three seasons, a full year with this one.
<!-- tui -->
Highlight one and press `e` to plan it the same way. `a` plans the first free one, and `d` clears one.
<!-- /tui -->

The form's **Season** field moves a plan to another season of the year. See [Seasons Ahead](help:section-char-ahead).

**Long work.** Set the form's **For** to **Until it's done**, or **2 seasons** to **4 seasons**. See [For several seasons](help:plan-season-repeat).

**Teaching and assistants.** Saving Be taught, Be trained, Teach with a Student, or lab work with an Assistant plans the other person's season too. See [Paired plans](help:plan-season-pairs).

### Finding an activity
<!-- help: guide-seasons-find -->

There are many activities, more with each sourcebook in play, so the Activity list can be narrowed.

<!-- tui -->
1. **Open the list.** In the Plan Season form, press Enter on **Activity**. The character's **Recent** activities come first, then every activity under its Type.
2. **Pick a Type.** Press F2 until the Type you want is shown above the list, such as **Study** or **Work & trade**. F2 past the last Type shows them all again. Or Tab to **Type** or **Book** and choose one.
3. **Type part of the name.** For example `read`, to narrow it further.
4. **Choose one.** Highlight an activity with the arrow keys to see its summary and rule under the list. Press Enter to choose it.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
1. **Open the list.** In the Plan Season form, click **Activity**. The character's **Recent** activities come first, then every activity under its Type.
2. **Pick a Type.** Choose one above the list, such as **Study** or **Work & trade**, or a **Book**.
3. **Type part of the name.** For example `read`, to narrow it further.
4. **Choose one.** Click an activity to see its summary and rule beside the list. Double-click it, or click **Choose**.
<!-- /gui -->

The laboratory's **Lab work** list works the same way, with its own Types: Inventing, Enchanting, Familiar, Texts and vis, and The laboratory.

### Planning lab work
<!-- help: guide-seasons-lab -->

The laboratory needs a magus as its owner before anything can be planned.

<!-- tui -->
1. **Open the laboratory.** In the tree, open the covenant's **Laboratories** group and choose the magus's laboratory. Its **Planning** tab (`1`) opens first.
2. **Choose the season.** The list shows the owner's seasons from the current one on, with "nothing planned" against a free season.
3. **Start the work.** Press `e` to plan lab work for that season, or `a` to invent a new spell from a blank design. Either opens the [Invent Spell screen](help:invent-spell).
4. **Choose the Lab work.** Pick it from its list grouped by Type (see [Finding an activity](help:guide-seasons-find)). The screen then asks only for what that work needs, and the Lab Total and the When panel update as you type.
5. **Schedule it.** Press **Schedule** (see [Scheduling](help:invent-spell-schedule)).

`d` clears the chosen season's plan, and any later seasons scheduled to continue it, after asking.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
1. **Open the laboratory.** On the covenant's page, choose **Laboratories** in the rail and click the magus's laboratory.
2. **Choose the season.** Choose **Seasons** in the rail. Its **Planning** list shows the owner's seasons from the current one on, with "nothing planned" against a free season. Click the season.
3. **Start the work.** Click **Plan** to plan lab work for that season. The lab work form (see [Invent Spell screen](help:invent-spell)) opens in a window of its own, and grows as the work you choose asks for more.
4. **Or invent a spell.** Click **New Spell** instead. It first asks for a spell to start from, the magus's own known spells first, or "A new spell (a blank design)", then opens the same form. The form's **Start from** changes it later.
5. **Choose the Lab work.** Pick it from its list grouped by Type (see [Finding an activity](help:guide-seasons-find)). The form then asks only for what that work needs. The line under the fields shows the Lab Total and when the work is done, updated as you type.
6. **Schedule it.** Click **Schedule** (see [Scheduling](help:invent-spell-schedule)).

**Clear** clears the clicked season's plan, and any later seasons scheduled to continue it, after asking.
<!-- /gui -->

### Advancing the season
<!-- help: guide-seasons-advance -->

<!-- tui -->
1. **Press `t`.** It works from anywhere in the workspace. The **Advance Time** dialog asks whether to advance from this season to the next.
2. **Read the summary** (see [The confirmation](help:advance-season-confirm)). To fix something, choose Cancel, change the plan and press `t` again.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
1. **Click Advance Season.** The card is at the foot of the chapters on the left, under the present season's medallion, name and year, on every page of the saga that has chapters. A question asks whether to advance from this season to the next.
2. **Read the summary** (see [The confirmation](help:advance-season-confirm)). To fix something, choose Cancel, change the plan and click Advance Season again.
<!-- /gui -->
3. **Choose Advance.** The saga is backed up first (see [How the season is applied](help:advance-season-apply)).
4. **Answer any questions.** See [Questions along the way](help:advance-season-questions).

### Reading the results
<!-- help: guide-seasons-results -->

<!-- tui -->
1. **Read the summary.** When anything happened, the [Season Summary](help:season-summary) ("The Season's Results") opens. It has one line per person, grouped by covenant, with the activity and what it gave, or why a plan couldn't be carried out. Press Enter, Escape or Close to close it.
2. **Check the date.** A message names the new date and whose seasons were applied, and the date in the header moves on.
3. **Read the Diaries.** Each character's **Diary** tab has an entry for the season. So does the laboratory's Diary tab for lab work, and the covenant's Diary tab for vis harvests.
4. **Browse past seasons.** With Keep season records on in the saga's settings, the season is also kept under [Seasons](help:seasons) in the tree. Choose it and press Enter to browse past seasons, newest first.

The season's plans are removed once applied, so every This Season section starts empty again. Lab work that takes several seasons stays scheduled on the laboratory's Planning tab.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
1. **Read the summary.** When anything happened, the saga's [Seasons](help:seasons) page opens on its **Just played** chapter (the [Season Summary](help:season-summary)). It has one row per person, grouped by covenant, with the activity and what it gave, or why a plan couldn't be carried out. When nothing happened, a message says so instead.
2. **Check the date.** The date on the Advance Season button moves on.
3. **Read the Diaries.** Each character's **Diary** chapter has an entry for the season. So does the Diary in the laboratory's Seasons chapter for lab work, and the covenant's Diary chapter for vis harvests.
4. **Browse past seasons.** With Keep season records on in the saga's settings, the season is also kept on the [Seasons](help:seasons) page. Later, open the saga's **Seasons** chapter and click **The Seasons Played**. The rail lists them newest first.

The season's plans are removed once applied, so every This Season section starts empty again. Lab work that takes several seasons stays scheduled in the laboratory's Planning list.
<!-- /gui -->

### Winter and the end of the year
<!-- help: guide-seasons-winter -->

Advancing Winter also closes the year.

<!-- tui -->
1. **Settle any deficit.** If a covenant spends more than it earns, the app asks whether to advance anyway. No leaves the date alone, so you can balance the books on the covenant's [Economics tab](help:tab-cov-economics) and advance again. Advance anyway rolls the year over.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
1. **Settle any deficit.** If a covenant spends more than it earns, the app asks whether to advance anyway. No leaves the date alone, so you can balance the books in the covenant's [Treasury](help:tab-cov-economics) chapter and advance again. Advance anyway rolls the year over.
<!-- /gui -->
2. **Read the year's changes** in the Season Summary and each Diary (see [Winter: the end of the year](help:advance-season-winter)).
3. **Check the Wealth.** It takes the year's surplus or deficit (see [Seasons](help:tab-cov-seasons)).

### Undoing a mistake
<!-- help: guide-seasons-undo -->

<!-- tui -->
- **The date only.** Choose the saga, the top node of the tree, press `m` for More and choose **Set Date**. Set the season and year and press Set. See [More on the saga](help:saga-more).
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
- **The date only.** On the saga's page, choose **Seasons** in the rail and click **Set Date** beside The Date. Set the season and year and choose Set.
<!-- /gui -->
  This moves the date without undoing anything the season did, and Wealth added at a year end isn't taken back.
<!-- tui -->
- **The whole season.** Choose the saga, press `m` for More and choose **Undo Last Season**.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
- **The whole season.** On the saga's page, choose **Seasons** in the rail and click **Undo Last Season** beside Set Date.
<!-- /gui -->
  It puts the whole saga back as it was before the last Advance Season, with the date, Wealth, Diaries and every result. It asks first, and names the records anyone changed since, as those changes go too. Each undo goes back one more advance, up to five, and a downtime run undoes as one. It isn't offered once Set Date has moved the date since. A storyguide who joined from their own computer can use it too, and the host's computer, which keeps the backups, does it.
- **Undoing an undo.** The saga as it was just before the undo is kept as `<name>.undone.json` in `sagas/backups` in your save folder. With the app closed, copy it over the saga's file in `sagas` and rename it to the saga's file name, `<name>.json`.

### What the app does for you
<!-- help: guide-seasons-auto -->

**You type:** the plans, the grogs' yearly updates, any dice rolled at the table, and your answers when advancing.

**The app works out:** everything else above: each plan's result, the dice left blank, the Diaries, the season record, and the year's end.

### Telling the covenant's history
<!-- help: guide-covenant-history -->

<!-- tui -->
The [History tab](help:tab-cov-history) gathers everything dated about the covenant into one timeline.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
The [History](help:tab-cov-history) chapter gathers everything dated about the covenant into one timeline.
<!-- /gui -->
It reads the covenant's Diary, the diaries of its place and the places and records inside it, and Ahead's one-off entries for it.

1. **Place the covenant.** Its place, set in Edit Covenant, is what the History reads from. That means its diary, the diaries of the places inside it, and those of the factions and people placed there.
2. **Name its eras.** Add an era on the Eras list for each stretch of years worth a heading, such as The Founders' years, 1115, or The breaking, 1200. Each runs to the next.
3. **Prune it.** Where two records tell the same event in different words, choose the one you don't want and Delete it. It's left out of the history, and stays where it's written. Entries with the same date and words already show as one row.
4. **Put one back.** Tick Show what's left out in the What It Shows block's Edit. Delete on a left-out entry at the foot puts it back.
<!-- tui -->
5. **Read one record's story.** Hide the other records' entries by unticking them in the What It Shows block's Edit.

5. **Read one record's story.** Hide the other records' entries by clicking their chips above the list, or unticking them in the What It Shows block's Edit.
