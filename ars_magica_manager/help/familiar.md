# Familiar sheet
<!-- help: familiar -->

The familiar sheet holds a magus's familiar: a magical creature bound to the
magus (Ars Magica Definitive Edition, Chapter 8: Laboratory, Familiars). A
<!-- tui -->
magus has one familiar at a time, bound with Bind Familiar in New (`n`).
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
magus has one familiar at a time, bound with the **Bind Familiar** card on
the magus's sheet or the **Bind Familiar** button beside Planning in their
laboratory's Seasons chapter.
<!-- /gui -->
Binding gives both the magus and the familiar the True Friend Virtue and a
Loyal (Partner) +3 Personality Trait.

<!-- tui -->
To remove a familiar, press `d` on its tree node or its Familiar Info panel.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
To remove a familiar, use **Delete** in its page's **Manage** chapter.
<!-- /gui -->
After a confirmation it is deleted and the bond broken: the magus loses the
True Friend Virtue and Loyal (Partner) Trait the bond gave them, and any
planned seasons of binding or empowering it. The magus's sheet is shown
afterwards.

<!-- tui -->
The Familiar Info panel stays on screen above the tabs whichever tab is
open, and the tabs open with `1` to `5`. On a tab with a list, the details
panel at the bottom shows the chosen row, including the rules text of a
Virtue or Flaw.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
The header, with the familiar's name, its magus, its Size, Wound
Multiplier, Base Soak, Magic Resistance and Magic Might, and the
Characteristic cards under it stay on the page whichever chapter is open.
The rail on the left lists the chapters: Identity & Cords, Virtues & Flaws,
Combat & Health, Abilities, Bond Powers and, last, Manage. Clicking a row
or card shows it in the inspector on the right, including the rules text of
a Virtue or Flaw.
<!-- /gui -->
Most of a familiar's figures are typed in as the rules give
them, rather than worked out by the app.

**See also:** [How to bind and keep a familiar](help:guide-familiars)

### Familiar Info
<!-- help: section-fam-info -->

<!-- tui -->
The familiar's Magus Partner, set when it was bound, and what you type with
`e`: its name, Species / Description, the season and year it was bound, its
Magic Might Score and current Might Pool, and its Size and Wound Multiplier.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
The familiar's Magus Partner, set when it was bound, and what you type with
the header's **Edit** button: its name, Species / Description, the season
and year it was bound, its Magic Might Score and current Might Pool, and its
Size and Wound Multiplier. The seal in the header shows the Magic Might.
Its Characteristics, Base Soak and Magic Resistance have their own button:
see [Characteristics](help:tab-fam-characteristics).
<!-- /gui -->

## Identity & Cords tab
<!-- help: tab-fam-identity -->

<!-- tui -->
The bond between familiar and magus, and who the familiar is: its Virtues
and Flaws, Personality Traits and Reputations.

**You type:** the details of the binding, the three Cord scores, the traits
and reputations; Virtues and Flaws are picked from the catalog.

**The app works out:** the point cost of each Cord score, and the points the
Virtues and Flaws come to.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
The bond between familiar and magus, its three Cords, each a card in its
metal, and the familiar's Personality Traits and Reputations.

**You type:** the details of the binding, the three Cord scores, the traits
and reputations.

**The app works out:** the point cost of each Cord score.
<!-- /gui -->

**See also:** [Abilities tab](help:tab-fam-abilities),
[Bond Powers tab](help:tab-fam-powers),
[Character sheet](help:character), [How to bind and keep a familiar](help:guide-familiars)

### Virtues
<!-- help: section-fam-vf-virtues; tui -->

The familiar's Virtues, each with its tier, and the points they come to in
the title. As on a character, Virtues can only be added and deleted, not
edited: `a` opens the catalog on its Virtues.

### Flaws
<!-- help: section-fam-vf-flaws; tui -->

The familiar's Flaws, each with its tier, and the points they come to in the
title. Flaws can only be added and deleted, not edited: `a` opens the
catalog on its Flaws.

### Personality Traits
<!-- help: section-fam-personality-traits -->

The familiar's Personality Traits, each a name and a score that you type.
The Loyal (Partner) trait comes from the bond.

### Reputations
<!-- help: section-fam-reputations -->

The familiar's Reputations, each a name and a score that you type.

## Virtues & Flaws tab
<!-- help: tab-fam-vf; gui -->

The familiar's Virtues and Flaws, side by side, each a card; clicking one
shows its effects and rules text in the inspector.

**You type:** nothing; you pick each Virtue or Flaw from the catalog.

**The app works out:** the points each side comes to, in the section
titles.

**See also:** [Identity & Cords tab](help:tab-fam-identity),
[How to bind and keep a familiar](help:guide-familiars)

### Virtues
<!-- help: section-fam-vf-virtues; gui -->

The familiar's Virtues, each with its tier, and the points they come to in
the title. As on a character, Virtues can only be added and deleted, not
edited: **Add** opens the catalog on its Virtues.

### Flaws
<!-- help: section-fam-vf-flaws; gui -->

The familiar's Flaws, each with its tier, and the points they come to in the
title. Flaws can only be added and deleted, not edited: **Add** opens the
catalog on its Flaws.

## Characteristics tab
<!-- help: tab-fam-characteristics; tui -->

The familiar's Characteristics and its defences.

**You type:** the eight Characteristics, the Base Soak and the Magic
Resistance.

**The app works out:** nothing here; Base Soak and Magic Resistance are
typed in as the creature's statistics give them.

**See also:** [Combat & Health tab](help:tab-fam-combat)

### Characteristics

## Combat & Health tab
<!-- help: tab-fam-combat -->

How hurt and tired the familiar is, and how it fights.

**You type:** its wounds, its Fatigue Level and its natural weapons.

**The app works out:** nothing; unlike a character's Health, a familiar's
Soak and penalties are not calculated. The penalty of each wound level is
shown beside it as a reminder.

## Abilities tab
<!-- help: tab-fam-abilities -->

The familiar's Abilities, in one list grouped by category.

<!-- tui -->
**You type:** each Ability's total XP and its Specialty; `a` picks a new
Ability from the catalog.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
**You type:** each Ability's total XP and its Specialty; **Add** picks a
new Ability from the catalog.
<!-- /gui -->

**The app works out:** the score from the XP, and the XP the next score
needs (Ars Magica Definitive Edition, Chapter 3: Characters, Advancement
Table). While the XP is typed, the form shows the score it buys and the
total XP the next score needs. The familiar shares its
magus's Language scores automatically, so there is no need to add them.

**See also:** [Abilities tab](help:tab-abilities)

## Bond Powers tab
<!-- help: tab-fam-powers -->

<!-- tui -->
The powers the magus has given the bond, in one list with a details panel.
<!-- /tui -->
<!-- gui -->
The powers the magus has given the bond, each a card with its Form's
symbol, level, uses a day and Penetration; clicking one shows it in the
inspector.
<!-- /gui -->

**You type:** each power's name, level, Technique and Form, target and
controller, uses per day, Penetration, vis spent and any cosmetic trait.

**The app works out:** nothing; a Bond Power's figures are typed in.

**See also:** [Binding & Cords](help:section-fam-binding), [How to bind and keep a familiar](help:guide-familiars)
