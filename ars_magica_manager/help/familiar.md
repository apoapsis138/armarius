# Familiar sheet
<!-- help: familiar -->

The familiar sheet holds a magus's familiar: a magical creature bound to the
magus (Ars Magica Definitive Edition, Chapter 8: Laboratory, Familiars). A
magus has one familiar at a time, bound with Bind Familiar in New (`n`).
Binding gives both the magus and the familiar the True Friend Virtue and a
Loyal (Partner) +3 Personality Trait.

The Familiar Info panel stays on screen above the tabs whichever tab is
open, and the tabs open with `1` to `5`. On a tab with a list, the details
panel at the bottom shows the chosen row, including the rules text of a
Virtue or Flaw. Most of a familiar's figures are typed in as the rules give
them, rather than worked out by the app.

### Familiar Info
<!-- help: section-fam-info -->

The familiar's Magus Partner, set when it was bound, and what you type with
`e`: its name, Species / Description, the season and year it was bound, its
Magic Might Score and current Might Pool, and its Size and Wound Multiplier.

## Identity & Cords tab
<!-- help: tab-fam-identity -->

The bond between familiar and magus, and who the familiar is: its Virtues
and Flaws, Personality Traits and Reputations.

**You type:** the details of the binding, the three Cord scores, the traits
and reputations; Virtues and Flaws are picked from the catalog.

**The app works out:** the point cost of each Cord score, and the points the
Virtues and Flaws come to.

**See also:** [Abilities tab](help:tab-fam-abilities),
[Bond Powers tab](help:tab-fam-powers),
[Character sheet](help:character)

### Binding & Cords
<!-- help: section-fam-binding -->

The binding's Arts, its initial Lab Total, the target binding level and the
vis spent on it, then the Golden, Silver and Bronze Cords with what each one
does. You type all of these with `e`; the app works out each Cord's point
cost from its score (Ars Magica Definitive Edition, Chapter 8: Laboratory,
Familiars).

### Virtues
<!-- help: section-fam-vf-virtues -->

The familiar's Virtues, each with its tier, and the points they come to in
the title. As on a character, Virtues can only be added and deleted, not
edited: `a` opens the catalog on its Virtues.

### Flaws
<!-- help: section-fam-vf-flaws -->

The familiar's Flaws, each with its tier, and the points they come to in the
title. Flaws can only be added and deleted, not edited: `a` opens the
catalog on its Flaws.

### Personality Traits
<!-- help: section-fam-personality-traits -->

The familiar's Personality Traits, each a name and a score that you type.
The Loyal (Partner) trait comes from the bond.

### Reputations
<!-- help: section-fam-reputations -->

The familiar's Reputations, each a name and a score that you type.

## Characteristics tab
<!-- help: tab-fam-characteristics -->

The familiar's Characteristics and its defences.

**You type:** the eight Characteristics, the Base Soak and the Magic
Resistance.

**The app works out:** nothing here; Base Soak and Magic Resistance are
typed in as the creature's statistics give them.

**See also:** [Combat & Health tab](help:tab-fam-combat)

### Characteristics
<!-- help: section-fam-characteristics -->

The eight Characteristics, with Base Soak and Magic Resistance below them.
`e` edits all ten figures in one form.

## Combat & Health tab
<!-- help: tab-fam-combat -->

How hurt and tired the familiar is, and how it fights.

**You type:** its wounds, its Fatigue Level and its natural weapons.

**The app works out:** nothing; unlike a character's Health, a familiar's
Soak and penalties are not calculated. The penalty of each wound level is
shown beside it as a reminder.

**See also:** [Characteristics tab](help:tab-fam-characteristics),
[Health](help:section-char-health)

### Health
<!-- help: section-fam-health -->

The familiar's Light, Medium and Heavy Wounds, whether it is Incapacitated
or Dead, and its Fatigue Level. `e` edits the wounds; the Edit Fatigue
button, or Edit Fatigue in More (`m`), sets the Fatigue Level. Unlike a
character, the familiar has no Soak adjustment here: its Base Soak is on the
[Characteristics tab](help:tab-fam-characteristics).

### Natural Weapons & Attacks
<!-- help: section-fam-natural-weapons -->

The familiar's attacks, such as a beak or claws, each a weapon with its
Initiative, Attack, Defence and Damage modifiers, typed in with `a` or `e`.
The details panel shows the chosen attack.

## Abilities tab
<!-- help: tab-fam-abilities -->

The familiar's Abilities, in one list grouped by category.

**You type:** each Ability's total XP and its Specialty; `a` picks a new
Ability from the catalog.

**The app works out:** the score from the XP, and the XP the next score
needs (Ars Magica Definitive Edition, Chapter 3: Characters, Advancement
Table). The familiar shares its magus's Language scores automatically, so
there is no need to add them.

**See also:** [Abilities tab](help:tab-abilities)

## Bond Powers tab
<!-- help: tab-fam-powers -->

The powers the magus has given the bond, in one list with a details panel.

**You type:** each power's name, level, Technique and Form, target and
controller, uses per day, Penetration, vis spent and any cosmetic trait.

**The app works out:** nothing; a Bond Power's figures are typed in.

**See also:** [Binding & Cords](help:section-fam-binding)
