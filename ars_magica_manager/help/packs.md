# Assumptions made for Guardians of the Forests: The Rhine Tribunal
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-rhine -->

The Rhine pack follows the book wherever this page doesn't say otherwise. Its founding years, ages, auras, Might scores and stat blocks are the book's. Where the book gives no value and nothing points to one, the record says "not given" and leaves it blank. You can change any of them in your saga.

**What it brings.** The pack is set in spring 1220. It brings the Rhine's ten covenants with the gilds they belong to and about seventy magi. It has the Black Forest and the great forests with their regiones, and the Rhine cities and princes. The Peripheral Code comes with nine open cases for the Tribunal of 1221, and the Curse of the Rhine Gorge is a threat. Durenmar's Great Library comes as Book records.

### Inferred from the text
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-rhine-inferred -->

- **Caecilius, Schadrit and Eule.** Age 160, from the book's "approximately 160" (Ch. 6, Ch. 8).
- **Gudrun Tigurina and Xavier.** Masters. The book says Durenmar has three masters from other Houses and names these two among them (Ch. 6).
- **Unnamed magi.** Durenmar, Fengheld and Irencillia have the counts the book gives, and Roznov's is worked out (Ch. 6, Ch. 8, Ch. 10).
  - Durenmar 4: three more Bonisagus and the unnamed third master.
  - Fengheld 7: the book's 23 magi less the 16 it names.
  - Irencillia 3.
  - Roznov 5: its three Ex Miscellanea, three Tremere and a Tytalus, less Igor Rastvan and Severicus.
- **Sites with several auras** take one figure.
  - The Brocken: Faerie 4 at the summit, where the slopes are 2 (Ch. 8).
  - The Rhinefalls: Magic 2 on the island, where the summit is 4 (Ch. 5).
  - The Source of the Danube: Faerie 3 round the pool, 5 in the water (Ch. 6).
  - The woods of Irencillia: Faerie 1, the lowest within the wall. The sancta's stronger patches are a regio level (Ch. 10).
- **The Four Seasonal Courts.** Aura Faerie 3, Might 30. Each court's aura peaks at 3 in its own season, and its Might Pool runs from 15 to 35 through the year. The pack takes the peak aura, and 30 for the rulers' Might score (Ch. 4, with Atlas Games' errata to p. 34).
- **Heorot.** A Spring covenant, as it was founded only in 1215 (Ch. 9).
- **The Tribunal.** 130 magi, from "approximately 130". The next meeting is spring 1221, at Walpurgis Night (Ch. 1, Ch. 3).
- **Peripheral Code years.** The book dates rulings Anno Aegis, and the pack gives them in AD, 139 years earlier. "A season at Durenmar" and "Ranks" have no year, as the book gives none (Ch. 3).
- **Diary years for loosely dated events** (Ch. 2, Ch. 3, Appendix B).
  - Terschelling turns to piracy in 1200, from "about twenty years ago".
  - Oculus settles in Lübeck in 1160, from "around 1160".
  - Durenmar's lean years are 1100, from "about 1100 to 1150".
  - The God of Truth is preached in 1210, from "over the last ten years".
  - Five things that happened "lately" are 1220. They're the Lusatia chapter, the theft of the Nebelkönigin, Caprea blinding Carolus's familiar, Fengheld's new masters and journeymen, and the Gorge ban running out.
- **Proxy sigils.** By the book's rule every master holds one retired magus's sigil and every archmage two, though it names only a few. Stentorius casts the sigils of Daria la Gris and Sicero of Triamore, and Severicus that of Hygwald Veritas (pp. 84, 109). The covenant totals are Durenmar 12, Fengheld 12, Crintera 7, Irencillia 5, Roznov 4, Dankmar 3, Oculus 3, Triamore 2 and Heorot 1 (Ch. 3, Ch. 8, Ch. 10).
- **Gild members the book points to.** A gild's magi are those the book places there: its leaders, the magi with a (Gild) Trained Virtue, and every named magus of Irencillia and Dankmar in the Elder Gild ("virtually all", p. 28). The rest are left to the storyguide. The Apple Gild has Oculus Septentrionalis, Triamore and Fengheld, its "substantial support" (Ch. 3).
- **Town populations.** Cologne 20,000 from "more than 20,000", Frankfurt 10,000 from "about 10,000", and Prague 3,000 from "about three thousand" (Ch. 5, Ch. 8, Ch. 10).

### Judgement calls
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-rhine-judgement -->

- **Oculus Septentrionalis.** Aura Divine 0. The book says Lübeck has no Magic aura and a "typical" Divine one, with no figure (Ch. 7).
- **Heorot.** Aura 0, as the book gives none (Ch. 9).
- **Triamore.** A Summer covenant, the app's default, as the book gives no season (Ch. 5).
- **Crintera.** At the surface of the Stubnitz regio, so it shows an aura of 0. The book doesn't say which of the regio's four levels is its home. Their auras are 1, 3, 5 and 8 (Ch. 9).
- **Other gild members.** The book names each gild's leader and the Houses it draws, not its covenants. The pack puts Durenmar and Fengheld in the Oak Gild, Crintera in the Hawthorn Gild, Fengheld in the Linden Gild, and no covenant in the Ash Gild. Roznov and Waddenzee are in none (Ch. 3).
- **Gild dues.** The book gives none, as a member owes the gild their vote and reasonable aid. So each gild has its agenda instead (Ch. 3).
- **The docket.** The nine quarrels the book leaves open in 1220 are cases for 1221, each filed in spring 1220. The pack chose whether each is a charge or a motion, and who brings it. Only Heorot's application and the Lusatia border dispute have had notice given (Ch. 3, Ch. 7 to 10, Ch. 12).
- **Lords' titles.** The app's highest title is Duke.
  - Dukes: the Emperor, King Valdemar II, King Otakar, the Landgrave of Thuringia and the Margraves of Brandenburg and Meissen.
  - Counts: the Count Palatine and the Princes of Mecklenburg.
  - Barons: the Lords of Cleves.
- **Lords' wealth.** The book rates none. The Princes of Mecklenburg are Poor, and every lord not listed here is Average. These are Wealthy:
  - The Emperor, the Welfs, the Count Palatine and Brabant.
  - The Zähringens and the Hohenstaufens.
  - Valdemar II, the Landgrave of Thuringia, Otakar and Henry the Rich of Nassau.
- **Coats of arms.** About 35 lords, churches and towns carry their historical arms. These come from medieval heraldry rather than the book, simplified to what the app can draw. Cologne's three crowns lose their chief, for example.
- **The Seasonal Courts on the map.** Inside the Hercynian Forest. The book leaves this to the storyguide and suggests the Westerwald, Odenwald, Palatinate Forest or Hunsrück (Ch. 4, Ch. 12).
- **Rudolph of Bonisagus.** A member of Fengheld, which the book implies but doesn't state (Ch. 8).
- **Magi of no stated rank.** Günther Lupus, Rudolph, Marguerite, Odorpes and Caprea are plain magi, with no Rhine rank and so no proxy sigil (Ch. 8, Ch. 9).
- **Libraries.** A covenant whose library the book only describes has one "library" Book record. Durenmar's Great Library has a record for each floor's collection, plus the books it names (Ch. 6).
- **The Curse of the Rhine Gorge.** One threat in eight stages, from the Praeco's commission to the seventy-seventh year. What happens when a stage is ignored, stopped or turned is the pack's own wording (Ch. 12).

### Corrections to the book
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-rhine-corrections -->

- **Durenmar.** Founded 767, when the Order was founded there. Bonisagus had settled there about 754 (Ch. 3, Ch. 6).
- **Dates where the book gives two.** The pack takes one, and each Diary entry gives the other too.
  - Merinita's disappearance is 792, not 785 (Ch. 10, Appendix B).
  - Dankmar is founded 1052, not 1053 (Ch. 6, Appendix B).
  - Rethra is abolished 1207, not 1202 (Ch. 3, Ch. 9).
  - Oakdell is founded 934, not 931 (Ch. 3, Ch. 12).
  - Grand Silesia is destroyed 1008, not 1009 (Ch. 3, Appendix B).
  - Ulm's freeing keeps both 1155 and 1164 in the text (Ch. 6).
- **Might scores Atlas Games' published errata reduce.** The book's printed figure and page follow each.
  - The King Fir 60 (90, p. 50).
  - The Glade of the Seven Waterfalls 50 (80, p. 51).
  - The Fane of the Founders 40 (60, p. 61).
  - The Ash of Nortorf 10, potentially 40 (15 and 60, p. 68).
  - The Teutoburger Forest 40 (50, p. 77).
  - The Apple of the Kyffhäuser 45 (70, p. 85).
  - The Linden of Neustadt 25 (30, p. 88).
  - The Holm Oak of Romowe 35, 20 while trapped (45 and 30, p. 99).
  - The Courts of the Seasons 15 to 35 (20 to 50, p. 34).
- **Stat blocks the errata correct.**
  - Felicia has Guile 2 (disguise as a man) and Chaos of the Angry Waves 30 (p. 23).
  - Tabanus's Trust of Childlike Faith is PeMe, and his Eyes of the Eons is level 10 (p. 21).
  - Philippus Niger has Encumbrance 1 (1), Chaos of the Angry Waves 30 and Flaws of the Weaponsmith 30. Shattering the Malicious Thurisaz, PeVi 30, and Kauno, PeVi 50, replace one Shattering the Malicious Rune (pp. 59-60).
  - Imanitos Mendax's Recollection of Memories Never Quite Lived is level 4 (p. 73).
  - Falke has Neptune's Imprisoning Arms 30, Appease the Queen of Winter 65 and Chaos of the Angry Waves 30 (p. 96).
  - Ioannes Acer has Encumbrance 2 (3), Piercing Shaft of Wood 10 and The Treacherous Spear 25 (p. 106).
  - Hilda's short bow is Init -3, and her grapple does no damage (p. 117).
  - Byrek has Fist Defence +6, Encumbrance 3 (3), and each Initiative 1 lower (p. 119).
- **Felicia.** Always spelled so. The errata correct the book's "Felecia".
- **The Oak Gild.** Murion leads it. Stentorius's familiar Oswald believes Murion has usurped his master's rightful place, as the errata to p. 84 say. The book printed it the other way round (Ch. 3, Ch. 8).
- **Danish is East Norse.** The book's West Norse is East Norse throughout, and the language table of p. 138 relates Low and High German to East Norse.
- **Text the errata remove.**
  - Hildegard of Bingen is no longer said to be a Larta of Jerbiton (p. 121), and has no part in the Divine reading of the Gorge's curse (p. 137).
  - Imanitos was apprenticed at "a powerful covenant of the Provençal Tribunal", not at Doissetep (p. 72).
  - The Exsules of Fenistal have Poor Student and Supernatural Nuisance (Faeries) (p. 114).
  - The Partitioning of 865 names the Tribunal of the West Franks and the Lotharingian Tribunal (pp. 15-16, 140).
- **Rhine customs over the core rules.**
  - Notice of a case is due a season before the meeting, as under the core rules, since Guardians of the Forests sets no other period.
  - The pack keeps the book's proxy sigils, where the core rules give each magus one vote. A master holds one retired magus's sigil and an archmage two, by their rank Virtues.
  - A meeting that would fall in a Grand Tribunal's year is held a year earlier. Grand Tribunals come every 33 years, in 1228 and 1261, so the meeting after 1221 is in 1227 (pp. 140, 143).
  - Journeyman, Master and Archmage each stand for Hermetic Magus (p. 20).
  - The book's rule that the higher rank wins a dispute certamen would settle is recorded as a ruling only (Ch. 3).

The un-Gifted Redcaps Boris of Novgorod and Horst are companions with the Redcap Status, not Hermetic Magus (DE p. 105). # Assumptions made for The Lion and the Lily: The Normandy Tribunal
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-normandy -->

The Normandy pack follows the book wherever this page doesn't say otherwise, including every printed age, aura, covenant season, founding year and stat block. You can change any of them in your saga.

**What it brings.** The pack is set in 1220. It brings fifteen covenants and the lines of fealty between them, about fifty magi, and the duchy's places, lords and churches. It has the ruined covenants, the vis tenures under the Perthean Compact and the Tourney. The Peripheral Code comes with its open cases, and the Tribunal's stories are threats.

### Inferred from the text
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-normandy-inferred -->

- **Auras the book calls "low" or "weak".** These are 1:
  - The caves of Pléchâtel and Kerbourg in the Grande Brière (Ch. 4).
  - The Dolmen of Bagneux (Ch. 6).
  - The riverbank and covenant of Nidi, both Faerie (Ch. 6).
- **Breton vis sources as tropaea.** The 1032 ruling makes Brittany's open sources tropaea. So these are tropaea: Lake Comper and the Faeries' Mirror, the Pléchâtel oak, Bréhat and the Seven Isles, Karreg an Tan, the Rostrenen hawthorn, Port-Goulphar, the Needles and St Cado's pillar (Ch. 4).
- **Sources as luctationes.** Under the 1039 ruling (Ch. 3), a source whose harvest needs skill is contested. These are the Singing Stones and Roh-en-Aod (Ch. 4) and the footprints at Bavacum (Ch. 8).
- **Covenants' own sources as seisins.** The Perthean Compact treats a source at the covenant as seisin. So these are seisins: Fudarus's vis from the spirits of Ushant, Oleron's surplus from the Apple Mile, Nidi's lilac grove, Atsingani's council tower, Lapis Crudus's Bleeding Stone and cliff vines, Confluensis's tidal whirlpools and Orchard apples, the Spider's Palace's sources, Requies Aeterna's graveyard and Atramentum Renatus's source.
- **Leases.** Norlanus's Laon legacy, "renewed for many years", and the Grove of St Lie's lease, with no end given, run until the Tribunal of 1221 (Ch. 3, Ch. 8).
- **Montverte.** 5 unnamed junior magi, where the book suggests four to six (Ch. 5).
- **Eboris.** 3 unnamed magi, as there are "always at least three" Sweated Shillings (Ch. 7).
- **Ghent's abbeys.** Founded 650, from "the seventh century" (Ch. 8).
- **Ages the book hedges.** Aristarcus looks 32, from "early 30s" (Ch. 11). Geirlaug and Celeres are 150 and Eulogius 130, the book's "150?" and "130?" taken at face value.

# Assumptions made for Faith & Flame: The Provençal Tribunal
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-provence -->

The Provence pack follows the book wherever this page doesn't say otherwise. Its names, dates, votes, auras, Might scores and stat blocks are the book's. Where the book gives no value and nothing in it points to one, such as Windgraven's aura or Stella Durus's founding year, the field is empty and the record says the book is silent. You can change any of them in your saga.

**What it brings.** The pack is set in 1220. It brings eleven covenants with their votes and about sixty magi, Dama the Praeco and Rebecca as the book prints them. It has Toulouse, Gascony, the Pyrenees, the Narbonnais and the Arelat with their auras and vis, thirty ruined covenants and Val-Negra's regio. The Albigensian Crusade is under way. The Peripheral Code comes with the docket for the Tribunal of 1221 at Glanum, and the Praeco's secret is a story.

### Inferred from the text
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-provence-inferred -->

- **Founding years.**
  - Tolosa Paratge 773, one of the four covenants of the first Tribunal (Ch. 2, Ch. 4).
  - Ostal des Exiles 1215, "created since the Tribunal of 1214" (Ch. 4).
  - Aedes Mercurii 1003, "just before the Schism War" (Ch. 6).
  - Miniata Sophia 1180, "roughly 40 years ago" (Ch. 6).
  - Ara Maxima Nova 1195, "established 25 years ago" (Ch. 7).
  - The Coenobium Rhodanien 970, as it already existed when the Birds of Camargue joined it then. The book still calls it "newly formed" when it destroyed Fraxinetum in 975, and the Diary says both (Ch. 2, Ch. 8).
- **Covenant seasons.**
  - Tolosa Paratge is Autumn, as madness "drags them deeper into Winter" (Ch. 4).
  - Ostal des Exiles is Spring, being newly formed (Ch. 4).
  - The Coenobium is Autumn, "such an Autumn covenant" (Ch. 8).
- **Auras given as a range.**
  - The Médoc: Faerie 1, the lowest of its dolmens' 1 to 5 (Ch. 5).
  - The Dolmens and Menhirs of Millau: Faerie 3, the middle of 2 to 4 (Ch. 7).
  - Val-Negra's surface: Magic 4, the top of the Black Vale's 1 to 4, as the vale is "the magical centre of the mountain" (Ch. 9).

  - Albi: a crusader garrison.
  - Castres: its consuls, for the Montforts.

  - Termes: a crusader garrison, with its lords returning this spring.

- **Unnamed magi.** The book's totals less the magi it names (Ch. 3 to 8).
  - Tolosa Paratge 4, Ostal des Exiles 3 and Castra Solis 11.
  - Aedes Mercurii 7, as it has about 18 and the book names 11.
  - The Coenobium 17, from "over two dozen" consuls.
  - Miniata Sophia 1, and Tres' Band and Fraxinetum Redux 2 each.
- **The Sorginak Covens.** 70 members, at least 61 unGifted and about 9 Gifted (Ch. 6).
- **The Jinn Corsair.** At the Gulf of Lion, beside the story seed of a corsair raiding that coast (Ch. 7).
- **Diary seasons** (Ch. 2 to 4).
  - The Tribunal of 1207 is in Summer, when the Tribunal meets.
  - Tolomae's March is in Spring, from "early 1218".
  - The Wizard's Wars at Tolosa Paratge are in Summer 1218, "weeks before de Montfort's death".

### Judgement calls
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-provence-judgement -->

- **Covenant seasons the book never states.** Castra Solis, Tres' Band and Fraxinetum Redux are Summer, the app's default. Aedes Mercurii is Autumn (Ch. 5, Ch. 6, Ch. 8).
- **The regio at Niaux.** The cave of the first painters has a regio at Magic 5, the main gallery's aura, as the book gives the regio none (Ch. 6).
- **Auras that come and go** are shown at their peak.
  - The Great Dolmen of Grandmont: Faerie 4, which rises only on midsummer night against the priory's Divine 4 (Ch. 7).
  - The Arch of Orange: Magic 2, which shows only at night when the town's Divine aura fades (Ch. 8).
- **Bentalone and Filii Sinapis.** Placed in the Toulousain. The book doesn't say where either stood (Ch. 2).
- **Lords' rank, wealth and manors.** The book gives none. Kings and the Duchy of Narbonne are Dukes. Great lords (Toulouse, England in Aquitaine, Castile, Aragon) hold 40 manors, and lesser lords 1 to 12 by their standing (Ch. 4 to 8).
- **Coats of arms.** The families' later historical arms, drawn with the app's plain charges. No covenant has arms (Ch. 4, Ch. 6, Ch. 8).
  - Toulouse, count and city: gules a cross or. It's really the voided, pommelled cross of Toulouse.
  - Amaury de Montfort: gules a lion argent. The lion is really fork-tailed.
  - The House of Baux: gules a star argent. The star really has sixteen rays.
  - Castile: gules a tower or, for its castle.
- **Covenant Boons and Hooks.** Chosen from the Covenants book's list to fit each description. Castra Solis, for example, has Aura, Manor House, Prestige, Veteran Fighters and Famous Resident (Ch. 4 to 8).
- **Threat stages.** The book stages only The Serpent's Venom. The pack arranged the book's material into stages and outcomes for the others: the Six Deadly Spirits, the Procrustean League, Tolosa Paratge's Civil War, the Coenobium's Hegemony and the Adulteration of Flambeau (Ch. 4, Ch. 5, Ch. 8, Ch. 9).
- **The Albigensian Crusade after 1219.** The stages for 1222 to 1229 follow real history, each marked "if history holds". They cover Raimond VI's death, Amaury's withdrawal, the royal crusade and the Treaty of Paris (Ch. 2).
- **The docket for 1221.** Every open case is filed in spring 1220. Where the book names no one bringing a case, the pack names these (Ch. 3, Ch. 5).
  - The Praeco brings the Presiding Quaesitor's succession.
  - Beatrice brings the deaths of Senex and Tibaut.
  - Gilbert brings the Ostal's recognition.
  - Magi of the Normandy Tribunal bring the case against Marie of Merinita's northern border.
- **Things the app has no record for.**
  - Covenants' votes go in their notes, and their opinions of one another are left out (Ch. 3).
  - A covenant whose library the book doesn't list gets one "library" record (Ch. 4 to 9).
  - The Mercurian rituals are Book records, not spells. The Circle of Light is in Val-Negra's notes, as is the vale's map of rooms (Ch. 3, Ch. 9).
  - The Templars' 100 commanderies and the Hospitallers' 81 holdings are their orders' member counts (Ch. 5).
  - Mystery cults and hedge traditions are notes on their covenants, magi and places (Ch. 3, Ch. 6).
- **Virtues the app's catalog lacks.** The Unicorn's Divine Animal and Divine Master are entered as Magic Animal and Magical Master, which work the same way (Ch. 5). Others, on Dama, Rebecca and several creatures, are named in the notes instead.

### Corrections to the book
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-provence-corrections -->

- **Dama of Merinita.** Born 993, so aged 227. Her stat block's age of 193 leaves out her 34 years in Twilight (Ch. 5).

# Assumptions made for Blood and Sand: The Levant Tribunal
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-levant -->

The Levant pack is built from *Blood and Sand* and its free web companion *Secrets in Sand*, which holds seven of the eight printed stat blocks. *Blood and Sand* is a Fourth Edition book, so its numbers had to be converted. The stats follow the Definitive Edition's Fourth Edition conversion guide where it applies. Where the guide is silent the pack made a judgement call, and each one is listed on this page. Otherwise the pack follows the book, and its names, dates, Might scores, libraries and vis stores are the book's. Where the book is silent or contradicts itself, the pack inferred a value from the text or chose one. You can change any of them in your saga.

**What it brings.** The pack is set in 1220. It brings the twelve covenants with their stances on the Crusade, the four great ones rebuilt with their Boons, Hooks and Aegis. It has forty-nine named magi. The eight printed ones are converted from the Fourth Edition, Wardat is a Hermetic Sahir with her bound jinn, Amber's familiar Luigi is there, and the rest are generated. Tau is the Praeco and Scipio the Presiding Quaesitor. The Treaty of Baghdad is in the Peripheral Code, and its renewal on the docket for the Tribunal of 1221 at Domus Pacis. The Latin states, the Ayyubids, the caliph and the Nizaris, the churches, faiths and military orders are neighbours. Jerusalem, Giza and the Levant's other places come with their auras and regiones, and the covenants' libraries with their summae. The book's jinn and creatures are in the Bestiary. The Treaty's renewal and Aedes Veritatis's secret war on Foothold are the main stories, with the Fifth Crusade at Damietta.

### Judgement calls
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-levant-judgement -->

- **The printed magi brought up to Fifth Edition creation.** Fourth Edition had no House Virtue and no native language at 5. Felix takes Guernicus's Hermetic Prestige (DE p. 46). Guillaume de Bourne takes Flambeau's Puissant Ignem, his better Art. Rufus de Clary takes Tytalus's Self-Confident, so his Confidence is 3 with his Tytalus apprenticeship. That's Italian for Amber, Arabic for Felix, English for Esther, French for Guillaume and Rufus, and Scots Gaelic for Spiral. Wardat, a sahira of Ex Miscellanea, has no Parma Magica or Magic Theory, as the book prints her (p. 67).

Tau is the Praeco by the 1207 ruling that the office goes to the eldest magus who can be reached, although Urbs Rubra's notes make Wraith the elder. ### Converting the printed magi
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-levant-conversion -->

- **The eight printed magi** ('Umar, Amber, Felix, Esther, Guillaume, Rufus, Spiral and Wardat) are converted by the guide.
  - Ages and apparent ages are as printed. The guide's added fifth only sets the age a character is built from, and these are printed, not built.
  - Characteristics, Arts, Twilight Points and Confidence are converted as the [Alps pack's](help:pack-assumptions-alps-conversion) are. 'Umar's Warping is 4, and Rufus has 1 more Confidence for his Tytalus apprenticeship.
  - Felix's Quaesitor Virtue is dropped. A quaesitor is an office of House Guernicus now, not a Virtue.
  - Their Virtue and Flaw totals are kept as printed, even where they no longer balance, as these magi aren't in creation.
- **Virtues and Flaws** take the catalog's tier. Where it has both, a Flaw worth −2 or worse is Major. Renamed ones take their Definitive Edition names: Elementalist is Elemental Magic, Silent Magic Quiet Magic, and Secret Vis Source Personal Vis Source. Those with no new entry (No Sigil, Old, Outsider Trained in Order) are dropped. Each sheet's biography lists what changed.
- **Abilities** keep their scores. An Affinity's points go to the Art it served, and Curse and Hex become Curse-Throwing and Hex, with their Virtues. A Fourth Edition total is a fifth of the Definitive Edition's for the same score. So the experience of a removed Ability goes to its replacement at the new cost, with no cap (DE p. 538).
  - Disputatio goes to Teaching.
  - Legend Lore goes to the region's Area Lore.
  - Storytelling goes to the best Living Language.
  - Scribe goes to Artes Liberales.
  - Forgery goes to Guile.
  - Read Lips goes to Awareness.
  - Certamen, which has no Ability now, goes to Penetration.
- **Spells** take the catalog's level and Art, so some move. Edge of the Razor, on Aedes Veritatis's swords too, goes from 5 to 20. A spell the catalog lacks stays as the magus's own, designed anew by the Definitive Edition's guidelines, with the working in its description. A General spell keeps its printed level. Scrying the past or future, impossible now, becomes a spell of the same level (DE p. 538). Each swap is in the biography.

# Assumptions made for The Cradle and the Crescent
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-cradle -->

The Cradle and the Crescent pack is built from *The Cradle and the Crescent*, a Fifth Edition book, so nothing in it had to be converted. Its names, dates, Might scores, stat blocks and vis are the book's. It's a region book, not a Tribunal book. Arabia, Mesopotamia, Persia and the Silk Road lie east of the Levant, outside every Hermetic Tribunal, so the pack holds no Tribunal, Praeco, Quaesitor, Peripheral Code or docket. Where the book is silent or contradicts itself, the pack inferred a value from the text or chose one, and this page lists each. You can change any of them in your saga.

**What it brings.** The pack is set in 1220. It brings Arabia, Mesopotamia, Persia, the Silk Road and the jinn's lands beyond them, with their provinces, towns, rulers and factions, auras, regiones and vis. The Order of Suleiman comes with its six families, and its thirteen houses of wisdom are covenants of no Tribunal. The Order's past is in the Diary of the Dar al-Hikma, and the yearly Majlis is in the calendar. The five named sahirs are companions of the Sahirs tradition. 'Arkhaman is as the book prints him, with his seven spells and Kharrat, the jinni bound to him. The Mazdeans and their fire temples, the Nizaris, the yatus and the Mongols are there, with 'Umar Khayyam's books. The book's jinn and beasts are in the Bestiary. Ten stories run from the succession at the Majlis of 1220 to the saving of Merv's books, eight under way and two dormant, beside the Fifth Crusade and the Mongol conquest, with the story seeds on the records they belong to. Before making new sahirs, turn on its four traditions under Traditions Offered.

### Inferred from the text
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-cradle-inferred -->

- **Auras the book's rules give but don't number.**

  - Zamzam takes the Haram Mosque's Divine 10.

  - Where the book gives only an era, its history takes a round year, and each Diary entry says so. Examples are the guides to Nur al-Din and Salah al-Din in 1174 and Kha'trazh's election in 1190.
  - Entries about the Majlis and the Crusades are in Summer, the rest in Spring.

  - The Stone Lovers are in the Najd, Iram in 'Uman, Lukman's nest in the Hijaz, Arzanjan in Greater Armenia and Takht-i-Suleiman in Jibal.

  - The qareen and the 'afrit of the Bottle have no place.

  - The House of Healing is Divine 4, with its laboratories in a Magic 3 regio below.

  - The book's story seeds are one line each on the record they hang on.
