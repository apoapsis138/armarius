# Assumptions made for Guardians of the Forests: The Rhine Tribunal
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-rhine -->

The Rhine pack follows the book wherever this page doesn't say otherwise: its founding years, ages, auras, Might scores and stat blocks are the book's, and where the book gives no value and nothing points to one, the record says "not given" and leaves it blank. These are the places where the pack had to infer a value from the text, make a judgement call where the book is silent, or correct the book. You can change any of them in your saga.

### Inferred from the text
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-rhine-inferred -->

- **Caecilius, Schadrit and Eule**: age 160, from the book's "approximately 160" (Ch. 6, Ch. 8).
- **Gudrun Tigurina and Xavier**: Masters. The book says Durenmar has three masters from other Houses and names these two among them (Ch. 6).
- **Unnamed magi**: Durenmar 4 (three more Bonisagus and the unnamed third master), Fengheld 7 (the book's 23 magi less the 16 it names), Irencillia 3, as the book says (Ch. 6, Ch. 8, Ch. 10).
- **Sites with several auras** take one figure: the Brocken Faerie 4 at the summit (the slopes are 2) (Ch. 8); the Rhinefalls Magic 2 on the island (the summit is 4) (Ch. 5); the Source of the Danube Faerie 3 round the pool (5 in the water) (Ch. 6); the woods of Irencillia Faerie 1, the lowest within the wall, with the sancta's stronger patches as a regio level (Ch. 10).
- **The Four Seasonal Courts**: aura Faerie 3, Might 40. Each court's aura peaks at 3 in its own season and its Might runs from 20 to 50 through the year; the pack takes the peak aura and 40 for the rulers (Ch. 4).
- **Heorot**: a Spring covenant, as it was founded only in 1215 (Ch. 9).
- **The Tribunal**: 130 magi, from "approximately 130"; the next meeting is spring 1221, at Walpurgis Night (Ch. 1, Ch. 3).
- **Peripheral Code years**: the book dates rulings Anno Aegis; the pack gives them in AD, 139 years earlier. "A season at Durenmar" and "Ranks" have no year, as the book gives none (Ch. 3).
- **Diary years for loosely dated events**: Terschelling turns to piracy 1200 ("about twenty years ago"), Oculus settles in Lübeck 1160 ("around 1160"), Durenmar's lean years 1100 ("about 1100 to 1150"), the God of Truth is preached 1210 ("over the last ten years"), and five things that happened "lately" 1220: the Lusatia chapter, the theft of the Nebelkönigin, Caprea blinding Carolus's familiar, Fengheld's new masters and journeymen, and the Gorge ban running out (Ch. 2, Ch. 3, Appendix B).
- **Proxy sigils**: every master holds one retired magus's sigil and every archmage two, as the book's rule says, though it names only a few. Stentorius also votes Triamore's two Tremere sigils and Severicus votes Hygwald Veritas's. Covenant totals: Durenmar 12, Fengheld 12, Crintera 7, Irencillia 5, Roznov 4, Dankmar 3, Oculus 3, Triamore 2, Heorot 1 (Ch. 3, Ch. 8, Ch. 10).
- **Gild members the book points to**: the Elder Gild has Irencillia and Dankmar, whose magi are "virtually all" members; the Apple Gild has Oculus Septentrionalis, Triamore and Fengheld, its "substantial support" (Ch. 3).
- **Town populations**: Cologne 20,000 ("more than 20,000"), Frankfurt 10,000 ("about 10,000"), Prague 3,000 ("about three thousand") (Ch. 5, Ch. 8, Ch. 10).

### Judgement calls
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-rhine-judgement -->

- **Oculus Septentrionalis**: aura Divine 0. The book says Lübeck has no Magic aura and a "typical" Divine one, with no figure (Ch. 7).
- **Heorot**: aura 0; the book gives none (Ch. 9).
- **Triamore**: a Summer covenant, the app's default, as the book gives no season (Ch. 5).
- **Crintera**: at the surface of the Stubnitz regio, so it shows an aura of 0. The book doesn't say which of the regio's four levels (auras 1, 3, 5 and 8) is its home (Ch. 9).
- **Other gild members**: the book names each gild's leader and the Houses it draws, not its covenants. The pack puts Durenmar and Fengheld in the Oak Gild, Crintera in the Hawthorn Gild, Fengheld in the Linden Gild, and no covenant in the Ash Gild; Roznov and Waddenzee are in none (Ch. 3).
- **Gild dues**: the book gives none, so each gild's Dues read "None: a member owes the gild their vote and reasonable aid" (Ch. 3).
- **The docket**: the nine quarrels the book leaves open in 1220 are cases for 1221, each filed in spring 1220. The pack chose whether each is a charge or a motion and who brings it, and only Heorot's application and the Lusatia border dispute have had notice given (Ch. 3, Ch. 7 to 10, Ch. 12).
- **Lords' titles**: the app's highest title is Duke, so the Emperor, King Valdemar II, King Otakar, the Landgrave of Thuringia and the Margraves of Brandenburg and Meissen are Dukes; the Count Palatine and the Princes of Mecklenburg are Counts; the Lords of Cleves are Barons.
- **Lords' wealth**: the book rates none. Wealthy: the Emperor, the Welfs, the Count Palatine, Brabant, the Zähringens, the Hohenstaufens, Valdemar II, the Landgrave of Thuringia, Otakar and Henry the Rich of Nassau. Poor: the Princes of Mecklenburg. Every other lord is Average.
- **Coats of arms**: about 35 lords, churches and towns carry their historical arms, from medieval heraldry rather than the book, simplified to what the app can draw (Cologne's three crowns lose their chief).
- **The Seasonal Courts on the map**: inside the Hercynian Forest. The book leaves this to the storyguide and suggests the Westerwald, Odenwald, Palatinate Forest or Hunsrück (Ch. 4, Ch. 12).
- **Rudolph of Bonisagus**: a member of Fengheld, which the book implies but doesn't state (Ch. 8).
- **Magi of no stated rank** (Günther Lupus, Rudolph, Marguerite, Odorpes and Caprea): plain magi, with no Rhine rank and so no proxy sigil (Ch. 8, Ch. 9).
- **Libraries**: a covenant whose library the book only describes has one "library" Book record; Durenmar's Great Library has a record for each floor's collection, plus the books it names (Ch. 6).
- **The Curse of the Rhine Gorge**: one threat in eight stages, from the Praeco's commission to the seventy-seventh year. What happens when a stage is ignored, stopped or turned is the pack's own wording (Ch. 12).

### Corrections to the book
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-rhine-corrections -->

- **Durenmar**: founded 767, when the Order was founded there; Bonisagus had settled there about 754 (Ch. 3, Ch. 6).
- **Dates where the book gives two**, the pack takes: Merinita's disappearance 792, not 785 (Ch. 10, Appendix B); Dankmar founded 1052, not 1053 (Ch. 6, Appendix B); Rethra abolished 1207, not 1202 (Ch. 3, Ch. 9); Oakdell founded 934, not 931 (Ch. 3, Ch. 12); Grand Silesia destroyed 1008, not 1009 (Ch. 3, Appendix B). Ulm's freeing keeps both 1155 and 1164 in the text (Ch. 6).
- **Felecia**: Dorana's apprentice is spelled so; the book uses two spellings (Ch. 3).
- **Rhine customs over the core rules**: the pack keeps the book's year of notice for a case (the core rules say a season) and its proxy sigils (the core rules give each magus one vote). Its rule that the higher rank wins a dispute certamen would settle is recorded as a ruling only (Ch. 3).

### For every magus
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-rhine-shared -->

- **Generated stats**: the 61 magi the book describes without a stat block have stats built by the creation rules, following what the book says they are good at. Each sheet says so, and you can import them as the book's sketches instead (see [How to start a saga from a Tribunal book](help:guide-book-packs)).
- **Assumed ages**, from rank and history, where the book gives none: Gudrun Tigurina 70, Xavier 60, Indulius 65, Günther Lupus 55, Rudolph 45, Clemens 65, Marguerite 40, Wilhelm Weiss 60, Garrinchus 27, Hassan El-Megrayhi 80, Odorpes 60 and Caprea 50. Each sheet says the age is assumed.


# Assumptions made for The Lion and the Lily: The Normandy Tribunal
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-normandy -->

The Normandy pack follows the book wherever this page doesn't say otherwise, including every printed age, aura, covenant season, founding year and stat block. These are the places where the pack had to infer a value from the text, make a judgement call where the book is silent, or correct the book. You can change any of them in your saga.

### Inferred from the text
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-normandy-inferred -->

- **Auras the book calls "low" or "weak"**: 1, for the caves of Pléchâtel and Kerbourg in the Grande Brière (Ch. 4), the Dolmen of Bagneux (Ch. 6), and the riverbank and covenant of Nidi, both Faerie (Ch. 6).
- **Breton vis sources as tropaea**: the 1032 ruling makes Brittany's open sources tropaea, so Lake Comper and the Faeries' Mirror, the Pléchâtel oak, Bréhat and the Seven Isles, Karreg an Tan, the Rostrenen hawthorn, Port-Goulphar, the Needles and St Cado's pillar are tropaea (Ch. 4).
- **Sources as luctationes**: under the 1039 ruling (Ch. 3), a source whose harvest needs skill is contested: the Singing Stones and Roh-en-Aod (Ch. 4) and the footprints at Bavacum (Ch. 8).
- **Covenants' own sources as seisins**: the Perthean Compact treats a source at the covenant as seisin: Fudarus's vis from the spirits of Ushant, Oleron's surplus from the Apple Mile, Nidi's lilac grove, Atsingani's council tower, Lapis Crudus's Bleeding Stone and cliff vines, Confluensis's tidal whirlpools and Orchard apples, the Spider's Palace's sources, Requies Aeterna's graveyard and Atramentum Renatus's source.
- **Leases**: Norlanus's Laon legacy ("renewed for many years") and the Grove of St Lie's lease (no end given) run until the Tribunal of 1221 (Ch. 3, Ch. 8).
- **Montverte**: 5 unnamed junior magi; the book suggests four to six (Ch. 5).
- **Eboris**: 3 unnamed magi, as there are "always at least three" Sweated Shillings (Ch. 7).
- **Ghent's abbeys**: founded 650, from "the seventh century" (Ch. 8).
- **Ages the book hedges**: Aristarcus looks 32 ("early 30s") (Ch. 11); Geirlaug and Celeres are 150 and Eulogius 130, the book's "150?" and "130?" taken at face value.

### Judgement calls
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-normandy-judgement -->

- **Covenant seasons the book doesn't give**: Spring for Atramentum Renatus; Summer for Exspectatio, Florum, the Spider's Palace and Cunfin; Autumn for Fudarus, Oleron, Eboris and Confluensis; Winter for Lapis Crudus. Nidi and Dragon's Rest have none.
- **Ruined covenants**: Magic sites, except Sinapis, which is Divine. Those the book doesn't place sit at Kerguntuil on the Rose Coast, Caput Capra in the Ile de France, Bibracte in Burgundy, Baiocassium and Baiocassium Minor in the county of Bayeux, Rotomagus in the Seine basin, and Branugurix at Mount Dol.
- **Atramentum Renatus**: placed in Burgundy; the book never says where it stands.
- **Realms for places with no aura given**: Faerie for the county of Bayeux, the Forest of Brix, the Morvan and Fontainebleau; Magic for the Forest of Lyons, the Ile de France, the Yvelines, the Sologne, the islets of Paris, the Forest of Orient, St Gond, the Temple of Janus, the pyramid of Autun and the Vacant Castle; Divine for the quarters of Paris.
- **How well known places and ruins are**: the book doesn't rate this, so the pack set each one.
- **The King of France**: a Duke, as the app's titles go no higher.
- **Coats of arms**: the book prints almost none, so lords carry their historical arms as far as the app can draw them: Brittany plain ermine (the later form; Peter of Dreux bore checky with an ermine quarter), Normandy gules two lions or, France azure three fleurs-de-lis (strewn across the field in 1220), Flanders or a lion sable, Champagne's bend without its cotises, Burgundy bendy without the bordure, the Templars argent a cross gules.
- **Story seeds as threats**: Chapter 10's story seeds are threats with stages and outcomes for when each is ignored, stopped or turned; those stages are the pack's own (Ch. 10).
- **Creatures' Fatigue**: the book gives none for the lutin or the Boar King, so none is entered.

### Corrections to the book
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-normandy-corrections -->

- **Anacrôn's spells**: Irresistible Pressgang is CrMe 35 and Twisted Spine Mu(Pe)Co 20, as their descriptions say; his spell list gives level 40 and MuCo 25 (Ch. 11).
- **Puissant Abilities and Arts in Iasper's and Rotgiers' blocks**: the printed scores include the Virtue's bonus, so the pack enters the base score (Concentration 2 + 2, Terram 6 + 3, Single Weapon 5 + 2) and the bonus isn't counted twice.
- **The Wizard's Mount and The Leap of Homecoming**: levels 20 and 30, the book's stat blocks, not the app's spell list's 35.
- **Florum's move**: 1117, the timeline's date; Chapter 8 says the tenth century, and the record notes the clash.
- **The Spider's Palace**: the book puts it both "two days north of Ghent" and "between Bruges and Ghent"; the record notes both (Ch. 8).
- **Slips kept, with a note**: Mnemon's age of 97 fits his history badly, and Skuld is "resident at Atramentum" before 1178, though Atramentum was founded in 1200.

### For every magus
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-normandy-shared -->

- **Generated stats**: a magus with no stat block in the book has Characteristics, Arts, Abilities and spells built by the creation rules, guided by the book's write-up; Iasper, Rotgiers, Anacrôn, Hervé and Baudouin have the book's blocks. You can import them as sketches instead (see [How to start a saga from a Tribunal book](help:guide-book-packs)).
- **Assumed ages**, from rank, where the book gives none: Andru 90, Constantina 30, Languidus 45, Marcus of Paris 40, Henri 40, Norlanus 90, Gulo 37, Helios 38, Iacula 40, Philip 45, Renaud 33, Myrina 40 and Porphyrion 160. Each sheet says the age is assumed.


# Assumptions made for Faith & Flame: The Provençal Tribunal
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-provence -->

The Provence pack follows the book wherever this page doesn't say otherwise: its names, dates, votes, auras, Might scores and stat blocks are the book's, and where the book gives no value and nothing in it points to one (Windgraven's aura, Stella Durus's founding year), the field is empty and the record says the book is silent. These are the places where the pack had to infer a value from the text, make a judgement call where the book is silent, or correct the book. You can change any of them in your saga.

### Inferred from the text
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-provence-inferred -->

- **Founding years**: Tolosa Paratge 773, one of the four covenants of the first Tribunal (Ch. 2, Ch. 4); Ostal des Exiles 1215, "created since the Tribunal of 1214" (Ch. 4); Aedes Mercurii 1003, "just before the Schism War" (Ch. 6); Miniata Sophia 1180, "roughly 40 years ago" (Ch. 6); Ara Maxima Nova 1195, "established 25 years ago" (Ch. 7); the Coenobium Rhodanien 970, as it already existed when the Birds of Camargue joined it then (Ch. 2, Ch. 8).
- **Covenant seasons**: Tolosa Paratge Autumn, as madness "drags them deeper into Winter" (Ch. 4); Ostal des Exiles Spring, being newly formed (Ch. 4); the Coenobium Autumn, "such an Autumn covenant" (Ch. 8).
- **Auras given as a range**: the Médoc Faerie 1, the lowest of its dolmens' 1 to 5 (Ch. 5); the Dolmens and Menhirs of Millau Faerie 3, the middle of 2 to 4 (Ch. 7); Val-Negra's surface Magic 4, the top of the Black Vale's 1 to 4, as the vale is "the magical centre of the mountain" (Ch. 9).
- **Who holds a town in 1220**, from its last change of hands: Albi a crusader garrison, Castres its consuls for the Montforts, Montcuq and Montauban held for Count Raimond, Termes a crusader garrison with its lords returning this spring, and Pujol (St. Tropez) (Ch. 4).
- **Unnamed magi**, the book's totals less the magi it names: Tolosa Paratge 4, Ostal des Exiles 3, Castra Solis 11, Aedes Mercurii 8, the Coenobium 17 ("over two dozen" consuls), Miniata Sophia 1, Tres' Band and Fraxinetum Redux 2 each (Ch. 3 to 8).
- **The Sorginak Covens**: 70 members, at least 61 unGifted and about 9 Gifted (Ch. 6).
- **The Jinn Corsair**: at the Gulf of Lion, beside the story seed of a corsair raiding that coast (Ch. 7).
- **Diary seasons**: the Tribunal of 1207 Summer, when the Tribunal meets; Tolomae's March Spring ("early 1218"); the Wizard's Wars at Tolosa Paratge Summer 1218 ("weeks before de Montfort's death") (Ch. 2 to 4).

### Judgement calls
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-provence-judgement -->

- **Covenant seasons the book never states**: Castra Solis, Tres' Band and Fraxinetum Redux are Summer, the app's default; Aedes Mercurii is Autumn (Ch. 5, Ch. 6, Ch. 8).
- **The regio at Niaux (the cave of the first painters)**: Magic 5, the main gallery's aura, as the book gives the regio none (Ch. 6).
- **Auras that come and go**, shown at their peak: the Great Dolmen of Grandmont Faerie 4, which rises only on midsummer night against the priory's Divine 4 (Ch. 7); the Arch of Orange Magic 2, which shows only at night when the town's Divine aura fades (Ch. 8).
- **Bentalone and Filii Sinapis**: placed in the Toulousain; the book doesn't say where either stood (Ch. 2).
- **Lords' rank, wealth and manors**: the book gives none. Kings and the Duchy of Narbonne are Dukes; great lords (Toulouse, England in Aquitaine, Castile, Aragon) hold 40 manors, lesser lords 1 to 12 by their standing (Ch. 4 to 8).
- **Coats of arms**: the families' later historical arms, drawn with the app's plain charges: Toulouse (count and city) gules a cross or, really the voided, pommelled cross of Toulouse; Amaury de Montfort gules a lion argent, really fork-tailed; the House of Baux gules a star argent, really of sixteen rays; Castile gules a tower or for its castle. No covenant has arms (Ch. 4, Ch. 6, Ch. 8).
- **Covenant Boons and Hooks**: chosen from the Covenants book's list to fit each description, for example Castra Solis's Aura, Manor House, Prestige, Veteran Fighters and Famous Resident (Ch. 4 to 8).
- **Threat stages**: the book stages only The Serpent's Venom. The stages and outcomes of the Six Deadly Spirits, the Procrustean League, Tolosa Paratge's Civil War, the Coenobium's Hegemony and the Adulteration of Flambeau are the pack's arrangement of the book's material (Ch. 4, Ch. 5, Ch. 8, Ch. 9).
- **The Albigensian Crusade after 1219**: the stages for 1222 to 1229 (Raimond VI's death, Amaury's withdrawal, the royal crusade, the Treaty of Paris) follow real history, each marked "if history holds" (Ch. 2).
- **The docket for 1221**: every open case is filed in spring 1220. Where the book names no one bringing it, the Praeco brings the Presiding Quaesitor's succession, Beatrice the deaths of Senex and Tibaut, Gilbert the Ostal's recognition, and magi of the Normandy Tribunal the case against Marie of Merinita's northern border (Ch. 3, Ch. 5).
- **Things the app has no record for**: covenants' votes go in their notes, and their opinions of one another are left out (Ch. 3); a covenant whose library the book doesn't list gets one "library" record (Ch. 4 to 9); the Mercurian rituals are Book records, not spells, and the Circle of Light is in Val-Negra's notes, as is the vale's map of rooms (Ch. 3, Ch. 9); the Templars' 100 commanderies and the Hospitallers' 81 holdings are their orders' member counts (Ch. 5); mystery cults and hedge traditions are notes on their covenants, magi and places (Ch. 3, Ch. 6).
- **Virtues the app's catalog lacks**: the Unicorn's Divine Animal and Divine Master are entered as Magic Animal and Magical Master, which work the same way (Ch. 5); others, on Dama, Rebecca and several creatures, are named in the notes instead.

### Corrections to the book
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-provence-corrections -->

- **Dama of Merinita**: born 993, so aged 227. Her stat block's age of 193 leaves out her 34 years in Twilight (Ch. 5).
- **Tibaut's death**: 1219, the date the Tribunal of 1221 assumes; the book also says 1214, which the Diary and the docket mention. The Presiding Quaesitor's office is vacant, with Beatrice the presumed successor (Ch. 3, Ch. 5).
- **Chief Quaesitors before Tibaut**: the Diary follows the history chapter (Protantus dies in 1208 and Tibaut takes over) and mentions the Gascony chapter's version (Senex until 1210, Tibaut acting from 1214) (Ch. 2, Ch. 5).
- **Tolomae**: "of Bellaquin", as in Chapter 6; Chapter 3's claim that the Coenobium was his covenant is noted (Ch. 3, Ch. 6).
- **The fall of Fraxinetum, 975**: the Diary gives both the history chapter's Wizard's War and the Arelat chapter's word that no war was declared (Ch. 2, Ch. 8).
- **Bentalone, destroyed 1207**: kept as printed, with a note that de Montfort's southern campaigns began only in 1209 (Ch. 2).
- **Names**: Dama's "Familiar Chains of the Faerie Slave" is her House's Faerie Chains of the Familiar Slave, "Cthonic Magic" is Chthonic Magic, and Rebecca's "Shapeshifting" is the Shapeshifter Ability (Ch. 5).

# Assumptions made for The Sundered Eagle: The Theban Tribunal

### Inferred from the text
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-theban-inferred -->

- **Founding years**: Aegaea 1020 ("about two centuries ago") and Polyaigos 1050 ("the eleventh century") (Ch. 6); Favonius 1190, as Memnos conceived it some fifty years ago and spent decades on the first ship (Ch. 6); Alexandria 1075, when building finished after Miphoria failed by 1063 (Ch. 8); Michael's Tabula 1207, the Tribunal after the fall of Constantinople (Ch. 7).

### Judgement calls
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-theban-judgement -->

- **Seasons the book doesn't give**: Summer for Epidauros, Erebos, Gigas, Hedyosmos and Oikos tou Eleous (Ch. 5) and Favonius (Ch. 6). The next Tribunal meeting is Summer 1221 on Delos.
- **Story stages**: the 1221 stages are in Summer, and the war with Nicaea and the Tremere appeal ("witnesses sought", 1226) have seasons the pack chose; later war stages follow real history, marked "if history holds". Which stories start active and which dormant is the pack's choice.
- **Houses**: Polydegmon is a Tytalus, as the book gives no House (Ch. 9); Evantia is a Jerbiton, like seven of her covenant's eight magi.
- **Native tongues**: Boustaphan, Allectores and Krum speak Bulgarian, Caterina and Satabus Italian, Wilrich High German; Boustaphan and Allectores keep Latin as their Hermetic language.
- **Tokens**: Maria Laskarina 5, Ioannes 7, Pythia 1 token and 1 service, Leontius 1 shard, everyone else none. The book says the register is in doubt (Ch. 3).

- **Covenant patrons** are Boons: Powerful Ally for the great patrons, Local Ally for Cyra, St Loukia and Hipparchus; Gigas's wolf Čovek gives none.
- **Rulers' titles**, as the app's nearest: Duke for the great realms (Epiros, Thessaloniki, Athens, Achaea, Bulgaria, Naxos, Candia, the Latin Emperor, the Emperors of Nicaea and Trebizond, the Sultan of Rum); Count for the Venetian Podestà; Baron for the Lords of Thebes and Negroponte; Landed for Lemnos, Rodos and the governors of Sinope and Amastris. - **From history, where the book names none**: Achaea's seat Andravida, Bulgaria's Tarnovo, the Sultan of Rum Kayqubad I at Konya, Lemnos's seat Myrina, the Podestà Ottaviano Quirino, and a Nicene governor for Amastris.
- **Manors and fealty**: Naxos holds 50 manors ("more than fifty lordships"), Negroponte 6. Athens answers to Thessaloniki, and Thessaloniki, Achaea and Naxos to the Latin Empire.
- **Placings and records**: factions with no stated seat, and Hosios Loukas and Rufinianae, are placed by the pack; a covenant whose books the book doesn't name has one "library" record; Ionnia's two summae and tractatus have no level or quality, as the book gives none; no coats of arms are given.

### Corrections to the book
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-theban-corrections -->

- **Rulers**: the Emperor of Nicaea is Theodore I Laskaris, not Theodore II; the Tsar of Bulgaria is Ivan Asen II, who took the throne from Boril in 1218; Theodore of Epiros, called both Doukas and Komnenos, is Theodore Komnenos Doukas.
- **Xylinites**: an event the book once dates 1205 is 1204.
- **Spellings and people**: Eudoxia (once Euxodia), Bobila (once Boblia); Nonna is the twin and Nanno the craftsman, where the book mixes them; Ziper is a magus, "he" throughout.
- **History**: Robert Guiscard was a duke, not a king; Antioch fell in 1098, not 1095; the Second Crusade has its historical dates, not 1143 to 1149. Each entry mentions the book's version. The Latin throne, which the book leaves empty, has history's regents noted in the text.
- **Alexandria's patron**: Fides, as the covenant's own section says, not the grove of myrrh trees of Chapter 3 (Ch. 3, Ch. 8).
- **Rules**: page references are to the Definitive Edition; the book's two new Virtues, Elysian Ecstasy and Olympian Pact, are added in the pack's own words.

# Assumptions made for Against the Dark: The Transylvanian Tribunal
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-transylvania -->

The Transylvania pack follows the book wherever this page doesn't say otherwise: its names, dates, auras, Might scores, vis and stat blocks are the book's. The book's open text has no page numbers, so references name a chapter. These are the places where the pack had to infer a value from the text, make a judgement call where the book is silent, or correct the book. You can change any of them in your saga.

### Inferred from the text
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-transylvania-inferred -->

- **Lycaneon**: Magic 6 on the mountain and the oppidum. The book calls the aura "exceptionally high (9)" only in some places, which warp and stand empty, and gives none for the rest, so the pack takes a strong 6 for the oppidum (Ch. 6).
- **Evredika**: Magic 4, the aura of the Devil's Throat, where its Tremere aedile lives; the oppidum is spread over many sites and the book gives it no single aura (Perperikon is 3) (Ch. 4).
- **Tablinum's Empty Halls**: Magic 3, from "Magic auras of 3 or 4" (Ch. 4). Tablinum itself stands on its Curator's Sanctum level (Magic 5), where the residents live.
- **The forts of Kezdö Válasz**: every fort is built in a Magic aura, with no strength given (Ch. 7). The permanent fort at Sarmizegethusa, on the old Dacian capital, is Magic 5, and so is Kezdö Válasz; Leona's Prokletije Fort is 4; the abandoned forts are 3, though some have turned Faerie or Infernal.
- **Gigas**: Magic 1, from The Sundered Eagle, as Against the Dark gives none (Ch. 4).
- **Places the book calls strong or names a realm for, without a number**: Mount Klek, "a powerful Infernal aura", Infernal 5 (Ch. 5); the storm wizards' ice cave, "a strong magic aura", Magic 6 (Ch. 9); the Rock Womb, a "powerful Magic aura" in January and February, Magic 6 (Ch. 4); Dobsinska and Ochtinska Caves, among the Aggtelek caves with Magic auras below Baradla's 5, Magic 3 (Ch. 3); the Night Streets of Zadar and Körösfö, Infernal 3 (Ch. 5, Ch. 6); Pannonia's first site, Divine 3, where a monastery destroyed the Magic aura (Ch. 2); the Sacred Glens of Perun and the Pirin Mountains, Magic 3 (Ch. 5); the City Below beyond the Gate of Eurydice, an insula of the Magic Realm, Magic 7, above the oppidum's 6 (Ch. 2).
- **Kezdö Válasz**: Summer, from its founding day, 12 August (Ch. 7).
- **The Scholomance**: founded 1125, thirty years after Jeremiah's entry in 1095 (Ch. 6).
- **Roughly dated history** takes a round year, and each entry quotes the book: the skirmish at the Shipka Pass 780, Tremere in the Roman dragon's shape 810, Akilina finds Tablinum's site 850, the zenodochia devised 1005, Coloman's law on witches 1100, the Fectores and Laniena founded 1120, the Roman dragon last seen 1165, the Teutonic Order founded 1190, Simium's letters to Polyaigos 1210.
- **Laniena**: two aediles, Lexander and Murat the Younger, by the rule that an oppidum with equal numbers of cives and others has two (Ch. 2, Ch. 3).
- **The Tribunal**: 82 magi, the sum of the oppida's counts; 42 are named.
- **The Transylvania Chronicle**: two Book records, quality 6 tractatus on Order of Hermes Lore and on the Code, from the book's rule that every fifty years of it counts as one of each; its nine sets and 90 volumes are in the notes (Ch. 4).

### Corrections to the book
<!-- help: pack-assumptions-transylvania-corrections -->

- **Sudden Well**, in Monica Ierne's spells, is PeTe 30 as written up, not ReTe 35 as listed; her Hermes Lore 3 is Organization Lore: Order of Hermes (Ch. 2).
- **The Definitive Edition's rules govern** the zenodochia's Warping (6 points a season inside) where they give a different count (Ch. 7), and certamen in place of Wizard's War is kept as a Code ruling (Ch. 2).
- **Creature Virtues**: the Vampire Tree's Passes for Plant is the catalog's Passes for Human, the Minor Vila's Human Form is Humanoid Faerie, and Silexa's Magical Creature is left out, as it adds nothing to Magic Animal (Ch. 2, Ch. 8).
- **Names and dates**: Pope Honorius III, not II, sends the legate about Bosnia (Ch. 5); Andrew II comes home from crusade in 1218, not 1222 (Ch. 6); the Landgrave, not Margrave, of Thuringia holds the Wartburg contest of 1207 (Ch. 1); the Teutonic Knights' invitation of 1211 leaves out the book's King Bela and puts Hermann von Salza's name right (Ch. 6); Albertus is "he" throughout (Ch. 6); Esztergom and Oradea are spelt so (Ch. 3).
- **Small contradictions**: Nicoleta's horse farm is on the Madara Plateau in Chapter 4, where the book's pointer says Chapter 3 (Ch. 8); Neculai is a socius of Domostron, as his own entry says (Ch. 7); Dominic of Tytalus stays a hospes while Coeris's aedile (Ch. 2).
