Source: "Lion rampant element.svg" on Wikimedia Commons,
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lion_rampant_element.svg
Author: Inductiveload, traced from an original by Jiri Louda.
Licence: public domain (released by its author into the public domain;
the original it was traced from is stated to be public domain).

Changes made: its shapes were merged into three layers (the silhouette, the black line work, the red claws and
tongue), flattened to straight steps and fitted to a new viewBox, so
the app can paint the lion in any tincture.

The other charges are drawn the same way, each from a Wikimedia Commons
file. Their shapes were merged into a silhouette, line work and (where drawn in red) claws or a door, so the
app can paint them in any tincture.
