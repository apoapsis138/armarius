    ▼ The Great Library of Durenmar

      Librarian: Andrus of Bonisagus  ·  Readers a season: about a dozen  ·  Exempt: Bonisagus,
      Durenmar's members

      Those who study from the Great Library must contribute to it. A contribution the librarian accepts
      (a tractatus, a new spell; not a common one or a mediocre summa) gives two seasons of study, more
      for an exceptional one; a season copying gives one. Lab notes and the Tribunal records need no
      credit. Books never leave the tower.
      A vis "donation" is quietly taken instead, never acknowledged (Guardians of the Forests p. 55).
