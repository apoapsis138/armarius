  Hibernia: Cathaigh and ambassadors. A covenant's magi vote only while it shows its cathach, the trophy
  it took when it was founded, and a new covenant must hold land, cattle and a cathach for a year to be
  recognised. The supernatural factions' ambassadors, confirmed at the Sacred Council, vote as magi do;
  its law is largely treaties; and the Coill Trí give the Order seven Gifted children at each meeting.
  It meets at the Hill of Tara, and certamen there allows no veto and no limit on who, when or how
  often.
