# Armarius

Armarius is a storyguide's tool for the paperwork of an *Ars Magica* saga:
its characters, covenants, laboratories and books, the seasons they spend,
and the world around them. This glossary fixes the words the app, its help
and its stories use for those things.

The *Ars Magica* terms are in "Book terms" at the end, each defined from the books with book and page cited.

## Language

### The saga and who uses it

**Saga**:
One campaign as the app keeps it: its date, its books in play, its settings
and every record in it.
_Avoid_: Campaign, game, save

**Storyguide**:
The person who keeps the saga: they see everything in it, its Secrets
included, and may host it for the troupe.
_Avoid_: GM, DM, referee

**Troupe**:
The players and the storyguide who play the saga together.
_Avoid_: Party, group, table

**Troupe covenant**:
A covenant the troupe plays, as opposed to the covenants of the Region.
_Avoid_: Player covenant, home covenant

**Player character**:
A character whose player is named, whichever covenant they belong to.
_Avoid_: PC

**Record**:
Any one thing the saga holds that opens on its own sheet: a character,
covenant, laboratory, familiar, book, Tribunal, neighbour or creature.
_Avoid_: Entity, item, object

**Hosted saga**:
A saga the storyguide opens to the troupe, so each player reads and changes
it from their own computer while everyone's changes are kept.
_Avoid_: Server, shared saga

**Remote seat**:
A Player's desktop app connected to a hosted saga over the internet, seeing
and changing only what that Player may.
_Avoid_: Remote session, client

**Invite**:
A single-use code, good for a short time, that the storyguide gives one Player
so their desktop app can ask for a Remote seat; the storyguide approves the
request before the seat opens.
_Avoid_: Link, token, password

**Device credential**:
The key a redeemed Invite becomes, kept on that Player's computer and
revocable by the storyguide without touching any other Player's.
_Avoid_: Session key, login

**Seat view**:
The saga as one reader may have it: everything that reader may see and
nothing hidden from them, the copy a Remote seat holds and what a Player's
export carries.
_Avoid_: Filtered saga, player copy, snapshot (that is how it travels)

### The table

**Session**:
One evening of play at the table, with its storyguide, who came, who is away and which character each player played. It need not sit inside one season (DE p. 528: a pulsed saga may play many Sessions in one season, or years between Sessions).
_Avoid_: game, meeting, sitting

**App session**:
One run of the program, or one sign-in to the hosted app. Not a Session.
_Avoid_: session (alone)

**Steward**:
The Player who keeps a character between Sessions and decides what it does in its downtime. Any character without a Player of its own may have one (DE p. 529 describes this for pooled grogs, and the word "steward" is the app's own).
_Avoid_: owner, handler

**Player**:
A person at the table, kept as a record on the saga. A troupe without sign-ins still has Player records, without passcodes.
_Avoid_: user, member

**Table-only Player**:
A Player with no passcode: named at the table, never able to sign in to the hosted app.
_Avoid_: guest, offline player

**Away**:
A Player who is not coming to a given Session. Their characters are not played that evening.
_Avoid_: absent, no-show

**Planned Session / Played Session**:
A Session not yet played, and one that has been. The next planned Session is the earliest unplayed one; its date may be blank.
_Avoid_: upcoming, past

**Solo**:
A saga with no Player in the player role: one person storyguides and plays any character (a storyguide-role Player record doesn't end it). Attendance, Stewards and absences are never asked.
_Avoid_: single-player

**Attendance not recorded**:
How a Session shows when it was made while the saga was solo and the saga has since gained players. It is not read as everyone being Away.

### What a saga draws on

**Sourcebook**:
A published *Ars Magica* book the app knows, whose Virtues, spells, rules and
other entries it can offer.
_Avoid_: Supplement (when the core book is meant too), source

**Books in play**:
The sourcebooks a saga uses; only their entries, rules and activities are
offered, and the core book is always among them.
_Avoid_: Enabled books, active books

**Saga source**:
A source the saga invents for its own entries, beside the sourcebooks, so
homebrew can be cited and switched in and out of play like a book.
_Avoid_: Custom book, homebrew book

**Catalog**:
The app's shared reference list of one kind of entry (Virtues and Flaws,
Boons and Hooks, Abilities, spells...), each entry citing the book it comes
from.
_Avoid_: Database, list

**House rule**:
An entry made for one saga (a Virtue, Flaw, Boon, Hook or Ability), offered
beside the catalog's own.
_Avoid_: Homebrew entry, custom entry

**Optional rule**:
A rule a sourcebook offers, or a supplement's different version of a core
rule, that a saga turns on; every optional rule starts off.
_Avoid_: Variant, setting

**Ruling template**:
A saga's own wording for a Tribunal ruling, with blanks to fill in when a
case is ruled on.

**Content pack**:
A Tribunal or region sourcebook's world, written out as records (its
Tribunal, covenants, magi, places, neighbours, stories and history) that a
saga imports to begin in a land already peopled.
_Avoid_: Book pack, package, module

**Export**:
A record, or a group of them, written out of one saga so another can import
it; a content pack is one too.
_Avoid_: Backup (which is the whole saga)

**Saga Content**:
The one place listing what a saga's pickers offer: its books in play and
sources, the catalogs and what it hides from them, its house rules, its own
spells and its players.

### Records and how much of them is written

**Sheet**:
The view a record opens on, divided into tabs and sections.
_Avoid_: Screen, form

**Tab**:
One part of a sheet, shown on its own; the desktop calls it a chapter.
_Avoid_: Page, panel

**Section**:
A titled part of a tab holding one list or one set of facts, with its own
Add, Edit and Delete.
_Avoid_: Box, group, pane

**Detail level**:
How much of a character or covenant is written down: a Line, a Sketch or
Full. Only a Full one is played.

**Line**:
The least detail level: a name and who they are.

**Sketch**:
The middle detail level: what play needs, as the books print their
non-player characters and covenants.
_Avoid_: Stat block (for the level), NPC

**Promote** / **Demote**:
To move a record one detail level up or down, keeping everything it already
holds.

**In Creation**:
The state of a covenant, laboratory or character still being built by the
book's steps, with the Creation Worksheet open on it.
_Avoid_: Draft, new

**In Play**:
The state of a record whose creation is finished, so seasons, aging and
upkeep apply to it.
_Avoid_: Active, live

**Creation Worksheet**:
The walk through the book's creation steps for one record In Creation, with
each step's budget and checks; it edits the real record.
_Avoid_: Wizard, character builder

**Library**:
Every book at a covenant: those it owns and those owned by its laboratories
and characters. It is a view, not a record of its own.
_Avoid_: Book collection

**Unassigned**:
Said of a character, laboratory or book that belongs to no covenant (or, for
a book, to nobody).
_Avoid_: Orphaned, loose

**Modifier**:
One effect an entry (a Virtue, a Flaw, a Boon, a Hook) has on one figure,
written as data on the entry; every figure is worked out from the modifiers
that reach it.
_Avoid_: Bonus, effect (alone), adjustment

### Seasons and time

**Season plan**:
What one character is to do with one season, locked in ahead of time and
applied only when the saga advances.
_Avoid_: Action, schedule

**Advance Season**:
Moving the saga on by one season: every plan for the season ending is worked
out from the saga as it stood, then all of them are applied.
_Avoid_: End turn, next season

**Season Summary**:
What a season just advanced gave everyone, by covenant.

**Season record**:
The kept account of one past season: what everyone did and what it gave
them.
_Avoid_: Log, history

**Yearly update**:
A grog's year, worked out once in Winter instead of season by season.

**The World Turns**:
The yearly step in Winter that proposes what the year brought beyond the
covenants (threats, places, fairs, loans, neighbours); only what the
storyguide ticks is applied.
_Avoid_: World update, world tick

**Diary**:
A record's dated account of what happened to it, one entry at a time; each
change the app makes is a diary entry, so it can be undone.
_Avoid_: Log, journal, history

**History**:
One timeline, oldest first, of everything dated that is written about a
covenant: its Diary and the diaries of what lies at its place.
_Avoid_: Diary (which is one record's own)

**Calendar entry**:
Something the saga knows is coming on a date (a yearly council, a Hermetic
year) that belongs to no single record.

**Needs You**:
Everything in the saga waiting on the storyguide, gathered afresh from every
part of it each time it is shown.
_Avoid_: To-do list, inbox, attention

**Ahead**:
What is coming in the seasons ahead, season by season, gathered afresh from
every part of the saga; Looking Ahead is its list.
_Avoid_: Forecast, upcoming

**Undertaking**:
Something the troupe sets out to do besides the seasons' plans: a journey, a
hunt, or a covenant's great work.
_Avoid_: Task, project (alone)

### The world beyond the troupe

**Region**:
The saga's world beyond the troupe's covenants: its Tribunals, the covenants
the troupe doesn't play, and its neighbours.
_Avoid_: World, setting, map

**Neighbour**:
Anyone or anything of the Region the troupe deals with: a lord, town,
church, university, faction, threat, place or custom.
_Avoid_: NPC, contact, location

**Place**:
Somewhere the saga's records can be (a forest, a ruin, a regio); a
neighbour with no attitude and no ruler.
_Avoid_: Location, site

**Custom**:
The land's lore rather than a company: what the people of a place do and
believe, with what keeping it asks and gives.
_Avoid_: Culture, tradition (which is a magical one)

**Faction**:
Any company with a will of its own, from a hedge coven to a gild to a
noble family.
_Avoid_: Group, organization

**Threat**:
A story that grows if the troupe leaves it alone, through stages that come
due by date, along a track, or by an omen count.
_Avoid_: Clock, front, villain

**Stage**:
One step of a threat, with when it comes and the clues that show it.

**Threat clock**:
A place's trigger tied to a threat: when it comes up and the troupe fails
it, the threat moves on.

**Dormant**:
Said of a threat the storyguide hasn't begun: it reaches no stage until
it is begun.
_Avoid_: Paused, inactive

**Story event**:
Something that happened to a covenant, character or laboratory and gives
rise to a story, kept as an entry in the covenant's Diary. It stays open
until it is Resolved or grows into a threat, with a year to revisit it.
The phrase comes from the books' laboratory results (DE p. 276).

**Story seed**:
A sourcebook's short outline of a possible story, which a content pack
brings in as a threat or as a line on the record it belongs to.

**Open story**:
A story event of a troupe covenant that hasn't been resolved yet.
_Avoid_: Plot hook, quest

**Story chapter**:
One dated account of what the troupe did, met or avoided when it took a
story up again.
_Avoid_: Chapter (alone, which on the desktop is a tab), session

**Revisit**:
The year an open story asks to be looked at again.

**Attitude**:
How a neighbour or another covenant feels toward each troupe covenant, with
the reasons it moved.
_Avoid_: Reputation, relationship, disposition

**Standing**:
A neighbour's attitude, the favours owed between it and the troupe, and its
links to other records, taken together.

**Favour**:
A debt of service owed between a neighbour (or covenant) and the troupe.

**Roll table**:
A storyguide's own table of what a place, threat, faction or lord might
bring, rolled on with a named die.
_Avoid_: Random table, encounter table

**Bestiary**:
The saga's creatures (beasts, spirits, faeries, demons), each with its stat
block; it belongs to the whole saga, not to a covenant.

**Creature**:
One being of the Bestiary.
_Avoid_: Monster, NPC

**Atlas**:
The saga's maps, each with a pin for the records that lie on it.

**Conflict**:
A battle, fight or siege the saga plays out.
_Avoid_: Combat, encounter

**Agent**:
Someone a character controls through a personal bond.
_Avoid_: Contact, follower, retainer

### Who knows what

**Secret**:
Something about a record only the storyguide should know, with a title,
its words and whether and when it was revealed.
_Avoid_: GM note, hidden note

**Known to the players**:
Said of a record of the Region the players know exists; one they don't is
left out of everything they see. The troupe's own records are always known.
_Avoid_: Visible, discovered

**Reveal** / **Hide from the Players**:
To tell the players of a record (which writes a dated line in each troupe
covenant's Diary), or to take that back.

**Player view**:
The saga shown as the players may read it, with Secrets and unknown records
left out; a signed-in player always sees it this way.
_Avoid_: Player mode, spoiler mode

**Bookmark**:
A record a reader has marked to keep at hand, kept on their own computer
rather than in the saga.

### Using the app

**Front-end**:
One of the app's two ways of being shown and used: the terminal app and the
desktop app. Each only draws what the core describes.
_Avoid_: UI, client, version

**Core**:
Everything in the app that is not a front-end: the records, the rules and
what each sheet and form holds.
_Avoid_: Backend, engine

**Terminal app**:
The front-end drawn in text, run in a terminal and served to players in a
browser or over SSH.
_Avoid_: TUI (in the help), console app

**Desktop app**:
The front-end drawn as a book, one page at a time.
_Avoid_: GUI (in the help), window app

**More**:
The menu of a sheet's rarer actions.
_Avoid_: Actions menu, overflow

**New**:
The menu of what can be created from where the user is.
_Avoid_: Create menu

### Building the app

**Feature**:
One of the app's lasting areas of work, named in the changelog, that every
story belongs to.
_Avoid_: Epic, module

**Story**:
One piece of work on a feature, small enough to review and commit on its
own.
_Avoid_: Ticket, task, issue

**Edition**:
A finished round of work on a feature, given a name and the version it was
finished in.
_Avoid_: Release, milestone

## Book terms

These come from the *Ars Magica* books, defined from the Definitive Edition
(DE) first, then the supplements and the errata, with the book and printed
page cited beside each.

### People

**Magus**:
A member of the Order of Hermes trained in Hermetic magic; "maga" is the
feminine and "magi" the plural. (DE pp. 6, 25)

**Companion**:
A central character who is not a magus, such as a knight, a friar or an
unGifted Redcap; each belongs to one player. (DE pp. 6, 25)

**Mythic Companion**:
A companion whose supernatural powers bring them near a magus in strength,
though outside the Order; they take a Free Virtue naming their kind and are
usually played instead of a magus. (DE pp. 25-26, 55)

**Grog**:
A minor, supporting character, often shared among the players; at the
covenant the word also means its full-time soldiers. A grog never has The
Gift. (DE pp. 6, 26, 190)

**Covenfolk**:
Everyone living at a covenant apart from its magi and companions: grogs,
servants, craftsmen, specialists, laborers, teamsters and dependents.
(DE p. 190)

**Familiar**:
A magical beast a magus befriends and then binds to himself by laboratory
magic; it keeps a will of its own. (DE p. 265)

**Apprentice**:
A Gifted person being trained in Hermetic magic by a magus, from the season
the master opens their Arts until they pass their Gauntlet, normally about
fifteen years later. (DE pp. 270, 273)

**Redcap**:
A messenger of the Order of Hermes, of House Mercere; most have no Gift.
(DE p. 6)

**Quaesitor**:
A magus empowered by the Order to investigate Hermetic crimes and bring the
evidence before a Tribunal; most are of House Guernicus. (DE pp. 6, 14)

**Hedge wizard**:
The Order's scornful name for anyone who works magic other than Hermetic
magic ("hedge magic"). (DE p. 6)

**Tradition**:
A school of magic with its own way of working it; many non-Hermetic
traditions exist, and House Ex Miscellanea gathers several inside the
Order. (DE pp. 203, 243)

### The Order

**Order of Hermes**:
The order of wizards spread across Mythic Europe, bound by an oath not to
fight one another or rule the mundanes. (DE p. 6)

**House**:
One of the twelve lineages or societies into which the Order is divided,
all but Ex Miscellanea descended from one of its Founders. (DE p. 12)

**Tribunal**:
One of the thirteen regions into which the Order divides Mythic Europe,
and the meeting of that region's magi, normally held every seven years.
(DE pp. 6, 22)

**Code of Hermes**:
The oath every member swears on joining, which sets the Order's structure
and the limits on what magi may do; also called the Hermetic Oath.
(DE pp. 6, 18)

**Peripheral Code**:
Every decision made in Tribunal since the Order's founding, taken as
precedent, though later Tribunals are not bound by it. (DE p. 18)

**Charter of Covenant**:
A covenant's written oath with its bylaws: what a member is owed, what they
owe, and what befalls them if they fail; once a Quaesitor notarizes it, it
is enforceable as part of the Tribunal's Peripheral Code. (Covenants p. 32)

**Gild**:
A formal political faction of the Rhine Tribunal's magi, cutting across
House and covenant, whose members vote together; it has no magical
purpose. (Guardians of the Forests p. 27)
_Avoid_: Guild (a mundane trade body)

**Mystery Cult**:
A body that teaches a mystery, a path to greater magical power gained by
Initiation rather than study alone; four of the Houses are Mystery Cults.
(DE p. 233)

**Initiation Script**:
The written procedure, like a Lab Text, that a Mystagogue follows to
Initiate someone into a mystery Virtue, whose ordeals and conditions give
it its bonus. (DE p. 388)

**Certamen**:
The formal, nonlethal magical duel by which magi settle a dispute or
precedence, fought with a Technique and Form both agree on. (DE p. 230)

### Covenants

**Covenant**:
A group of magi bound by an agreement beyond the Code of Hermes, and by
extension the place where they live, with everyone who lives there with
them. (DE pp. 6, 18)

**Boon**:
A feature that makes a covenant better, Minor or Major, paid for with
Hooks rather than Build Points. (DE p. 183)

**Hook**:
A feature of a covenant that leads to stories rather than weakening it;
each Minor Hook gives one point, and each Major three, to spend on Boons.
(DE p. 181)

**Build Points**:
The measure of a new covenant's resources (books, Lab Texts, vis, enchanted
devices, specialists), set by its power level. (DE p. 179)

**Loyalty**:
A covenant's Prevailing Loyalty: the average Loyal to Covenant Personality
Trait of its servants, specialists and soldiers, kept as loyalty points and
read as a score. (DE pp. 185-186)

**Laboratory**:
The workshop where a magus does the seasonal work of magic: inventing
spells, enchanting devices, binding a familiar and the like. (DE p. 249)

**Laboratory Virtue / Laboratory Flaw**:
A feature of one laboratory that helps or hinders work in it, Major, Minor
or Free like a character's, and shifts its eight Characteristics; the
lab's Size limits how many it can hold. (DE p. 286)

**Hermetic Project**:
A grand magical undertaking, such as building a covenant inside a volcano,
pursued by a magus, council or covenant through many seasons of laboratory
work and stories. (Hermetic Projects pp. 6-7)

### Magic

**Art**:
One of the fifteen Hermetic Arts, the five Techniques and ten Forms; every
magus has a score in each. (DE p. 206)

**Technique**:
An Art naming what magic does, given as a Latin verb: Creo, Intellego,
Muto, Perdo, Rego. (DE pp. 206-208)

**Form**:
An Art naming what magic acts on, given as a Latin noun: Animal, Aquam,
Auram, Corpus, Herbam, Ignem, Imaginem, Mentem, Terram, Vim.
(DE pp. 206, 208-209)

**Spell**:
A single working of magic; a Hermetic spell has a Technique, a Form and a
level. (DE pp. 6, 213)

**Spell guideline**:
The base level the books give for one kind of effect in a Technique and
Form, from which a spell's level is worked out. (DE p. 313)

**Enchanted device**:
An object holding magical effects that can be used without casting: an
invested device, a lesser enchanted device or a charged item. (DE p. 253)
_Avoid_: Magic item, enchanted item

**Lab Total**:
The sum a magus uses for laboratory work: Technique + Form + Intelligence +
Magic Theory + aura. (DE p. 249)

**Lab Text**:
A magus's notes on one effect he created, which let him or another magus
make it again far faster; Laboratory Text in full. (DE p. 262)

**Vis**:
Raw magical power held in a physical substance, attuned to one Art and
spent when used; it is counted in pawns. (DE pp. 6, 212)

**Vis source**:
A place, creature or thing that yields vis again every year, unlike a
one-off find. (DE pp. 180, 527)

**Aura**:
The strength, from 1 to 10, of one realm's influence over a place, which
modifies supernatural acts there; a place without one is mundane.
(DE p. 410)

**Realm**:
One of the four sources of supernatural power: the Divine, Faerie, the
Infernal and Magic. (DE pp. 5, 409)

**Regio**:
A place where levels of ever stronger aura share one spot of ground, each
hidden from the level below. (DE p. 420)

**Gift**:
The capacity to work magic; only the Gifted can learn Hermetic magic, and
The Gift unsettles the people around them. (DE pp. 6, 203)

**Warping**:
The lasting change a person suffers from strong auras or long exposure to
supernatural effects, counted as Warping Points and a Warping Score.
(DE p. 389)

**Twilight**:
Wizard's Twilight, a Hermetic magus's reaction to sudden Warping, in which
they leave the world for a time and return changed, or never return (Final
Twilight). (DE p. 228)

### Characters

**Virtue / Flaw**:
A trait most people lack: a benefit (a Virtue), or a drawback or hook for
stories (a Flaw). (DE p. 26)

**Ability**:
A learned skill, raised with experience over the saga. (DE p. 27)

**Characteristic**:
One of eight inborn attributes (Intelligence, Perception, Strength,
Stamina, Presence, Communication, Dexterity, Quickness), scored around 0
and fixed for most people. (DE p. 27)

**Season**:
Three months of game time, the span in which a character studies, works
in the laboratory and gains experience. (DE pp. 249, 375)

**Aging**:
The yearly roll from age 35 that may add apparent age, Aging Points and
Decrepitude, and can bring on a crisis. (DE p. 391)

**Confidence**:
A score and a pool of points an important character spends to improve
rolls; grogs have none. (DE p. 29)

**Experience**:
Points a character gains each season from what they did, spent to raise
Arts and Abilities. (DE p. 375)

### Books

**Summa**:
A book with a level and a quality, studied again and again until the
reader's score reaches its level. (DE pp. 179, 379)

**Tractatus**:
A short book with a quality but no level, which each reader can study only
once. (DE pp. 179, 380)

### The mundane world

**Lord**:
A noble who holds land (a fief) and is owed service, chiefly military, by
vassals holding land from him, with some justice over it. (DE p. 506)

**Gratitude**:
Points a character earns by serving their lord beyond what their station
asks, later redeemed for office, land or goods. (Lords of Men p. 7)

**Town charter**:
The grant from a lord that makes a settlement a town and sets its
townsmen's rights and taxes; it can be amended over time.
(City & Guild pp. 8-9)

**Guild**:
A body of a town's workers in one trade (craft, service or merchant), with
ranks from apprentice to dean. (City & Guild pp. 41, 43)
_Avoid_: Gild (a faction of the Rhine magi)

**Relic**:
An object, often part of a saint's body, holding Divine power, with a
Faith score, Divine Might and powers of its own. (DE p. 419)
