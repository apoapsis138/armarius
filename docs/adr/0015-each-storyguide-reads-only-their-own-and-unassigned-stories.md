# Each storyguide reads only their own and unassigned stories

ADR 0014 let a Storyguide-role seat read the whole saga. In troupe-style play with several storyguides (alpha and beta storyguides, DE pp. 529-530) that spoils one storyguide's stories for another who plays in them. So a record (a threat, a place, a lord, a faction, a creature, a character, a covenant or a Tribunal) can have an assigned storyguide: the host or a Storyguide-role Player. A storyguide in their storyguide role reads and changes their own records and those with none assigned; a record assigned to someone else they see only as a player would (no Secrets, Roll Tables, threat stages to come, or what the players don't know of) and cannot change. The host is no exception: there is no all-seeing alpha. The host keeps only who may join (the Players, their roles, Host for Players) and deleting the saga.

Every storyguide, the host included, switches between playing (a player's view of their own characters) and their storyguide role; a window opens playing, and each switch is logged.

A storyguide takes a record with no storyguide; only its own storyguide releases it or hands it on, so nobody can take another's record to read its Secrets. Deleting a storyguide, or making them a player again, leaves their records unassigned, readable by every storyguide: the host is asked first, with the records named.

The rule is kept by the app, not by encryption: the host's saga file holds every record whole. A Remote seat holds another storyguide's records only as the players have them, so while there are any, its storyguide leaves Advance Season to the host's computer, which advances every record from the whole saga.

## Considered options

- **The host as an alpha who reads everything:** the book gives the alpha a veto, but a host who plays in another's story would read its Secrets. Rejected.
- **The host may reassign any record:** simpler to manage, but the host could take a record to read it.
- **The role fixed, no switch:** a storyguide who also plays would see Secrets at the table.
